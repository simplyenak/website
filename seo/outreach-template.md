# Outreach Email Template — Link Insertion (comped-tour model, no paid links)

Voice: Passionate Friend. Short, specific, human. No flattery openers, no "I hope this email finds you well", no superlatives, no em-dashes. One ask, easy yes.

## Template

Subject: Your KL food tour guide, one addition

Hi {first_name},

I read your piece on {article_title} ({url}) and you clearly know the city. The {specific_detail_from_their_article} part is exactly right, that stall is on our route too.

I run Simply Enak, small-group food tours in Kuala Lumpur and Penang since 2011. We take people through Chow Kit, Pudu and Kampung Baru, to stalls like {vendor_example}. 5,000+ guests so far.

If you think your readers would benefit, would you consider adding Simply Enak to your list? Happy to give you the full tour on the house next time you are in KL, so you can write about it from experience. No payment expected either way.

Either way, keep writing about the real food scene here.

Maarten
Simply Enak

## Rules

1. Personalization is non-negotiable: {specific_detail_from_their_article} must be a real sentence from their piece. If you cannot name one, do not send.
2. Comped tour is the offer. Never offer money for the link (spam policy + entity risk, see playbook).
3. One follow-up after 10 days, then mark "no reply" in tracker and move on. No third email.
4. Target: P1 rows in seo/link-targets.csv first. Track outreach_status + dates in the CSV.
5. When they add us: confirm anchor text (branded "Simply Enak" for homepage, brand + keyword for tour pages), confirm the link is live, log date + URL in CSV, add to amplification tracker as third-party echo.
6. Rejection is fine. Log it, keep the relationship warm (they may include us next annual refresh).
