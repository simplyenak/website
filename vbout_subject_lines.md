## Key Principles

- Readers are **problem-aware**: they know they have a gap
- Don't reveal the solution (checklist) in the subject
- Lead with the gap, close with curiosity
- Keep under 40 chars for mobile
- No emoji (deliverability risk)
- Preview text complements, doesn't repeat
