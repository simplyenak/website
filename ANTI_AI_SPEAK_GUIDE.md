# Simply Enak Anti-AI-Speak Guide
**How to Write Like a Passionate Friend, Not a Robot**

---

## The Problem with AI Writing

AI writing follows predictable patterns that sound polished but fake. It uses:
- Formulaic transitions ("However," "Moreover," "Additionally")
- Em-dashes for dramatic effect
- "We're not X, we're Y" contrasts
- Corporate hedging ("It's important to note that...")
- Over-structured everything
- Zero personality

**Simply Enak's voice is the opposite.** You sound like a friend texting about their favorite food spot, not a marketing brochure.

---

## Banned AI Patterns

### 1. Em-Dashes (—)

**Why AI uses them:** Adds "sophisticated" pauses and drama
**Why they're banned:** Nobody talks with em-dashes. Use commas or split sentences.

❌ **AI speak:**
"Our tours—especially the heritage walks—showcase Malaysia's food culture."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Our heritage walks showcase Malaysia's food culture."
OR: "Our tours, especially the heritage walks, showcase Malaysia's food culture."

---

### 2. "We're Not X, We're Y" Contrasts

**Why AI uses them:** Easy template for differentiation
**Why they're banned:** Sounds rehearsed and defensive

❌ **AI speak:**
"We're not tour operators, we're your temporary neighbors."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Think of us as your temporary neighbors who love sharing Malaysia."
OR: "We're locals sharing our Malaysia, not tour operators checking boxes."

**Better approach:** Show the difference through stories, not statements.

---

### 3. Formulaic Transitions

**AI loves:** However, Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, It's important to note that...
**Simply Enak uses:** And, But, Plus, Here's the thing, Want to know why?

❌ **AI speak:**
"Moreover, our guides provide cultural context. Additionally, we accommodate dietary restrictions. It's important to note that tours run rain or shine."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Our guides share the cultural stories. Plus we handle dietary restrictions. And yes, we run tours rain or shine."

---

### 4. Corporate Hedging

**AI loves:** "It's worth noting," "It should be mentioned," "One might consider"
**Simply Enak uses:** Direct statements or questions

❌ **AI speak:**
"It's worth noting that Aunty Lim has been making laksa for over 40 years."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Aunty Lim's been making laksa for 40 years."

---

### 5. Perfect, Polished Sentences

**AI loves:** Every sentence grammatically perfect, no fragments, no casualness
**Simply Enak uses:** How you'd actually talk

❌ **AI speak:**
"When you embark on this culinary journey with us, you will discover that each dish carries with it a rich tapestry of cultural heritage and familial tradition."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Every dish has a story. Family recipes. Cultural heritage. Stuff that matters."
OR: "When you eat with us, you'll learn why each dish matters to Malaysian families."

---

### 6. Lists Without Personality

**AI loves:** Bullet points with parallel structure and no voice
**Simply Enak uses:** Lists that sound like you're talking

❌ **AI speak:**
- Professional local guides
- Authentic culinary experiences
- Cultural immersion opportunities
- Dietary accommodation available

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
- Guides who grew up eating at these stalls
- Vendors who've become our friends over 14 years
- Stories that turn sightseeing into cultural connection
- Vegetarian? Halal? Allergies? Just tell us

---

### 7. Passive Voice

**AI loves:** "Tours are conducted," "Food is served," "Experiences are created"
**Simply Enak uses:** Active voice with real people

❌ **AI speak:**
"Traditional cooking methods are demonstrated by our experienced vendors."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Watch Aunty Lim demonstrate how her family's cooked this dish since 1982."

---

### 8. Buzzword Overload

**AI loves:** Premium, luxury, authentic, genuine, world-class, must-see
**Simply Enak uses:** Specific details that show quality

❌ **AI speak:**
"Experience an authentic, premium culinary journey through world-class establishments."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Eat at the stalls we visit with our own families. The places with 40-year-old recipes and no English menus."

---

### 9. Generic Adjectives

**AI loves:** Amazing, incredible, unforgettable, unique
**Simply Enak uses:** Sensory details that let readers imagine

❌ **AI speak:**
"Enjoy an incredible array of amazing flavors on this unforgettable journey."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"Crispy roti canai straight from the griddle. Spicy sambal that makes your nose run. Herbal tea so therapeutic you'll want the recipe."

---

### 10. Third-Person Distance

**AI loves:** "Guests will experience," "Travelers discover"
**Simply Enak uses:** You and we, direct connection

❌ **AI speak:**
"Participants will have the opportunity to engage with local vendors and discover authentic Malaysian cuisine."

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**
"You'll meet the vendors we've known for years. Taste dishes our grandmothers taught us to love."

## The Simply Enak Voice Quick Test

Read your sentence out loud. Ask:

1. **Would I actually say this to a friend?**
   If no, rewrite.

2. **Does it have specific details?**
   (Names, ages, years, sensory words)
   If no, add them.

3. **Am I showing or claiming?**
   (Showing = stories and proof. Claiming = "best" and "authentic")
   Always show.

4. **Is there a human in this sentence?**
   (Aunty Lim, we, you, our guides, families)
   If no, add one.

5. **Could any food tour company say this?**
   If yes, it's too generic. Make it uniquely Simply Enak.

## Before & After Examples

✅ **Simply Enak voice:**

- Made it conversational

## Writing Exercises

### Exercise 1: Remove the AI Speak

Take any AI-written sentence and apply the fixes:

**Start:** "Moreover, our tours provide authentic experiences—featuring[em-dash] traditional vendors who've operated establishments for decades. We're not just tour operators—we're[contrast] passionate locals sharing cultural heritage."

**Your turn:** Rewrite removing:
- Em-dashes
- "We're not X, we're Y"
- Formulaic transitions
- Buzzwords
- Generic terms

**Sample answer:** "We walk you through the neighborhoods where we grew up. You'll meet vendors our families have known for 40 years. The stalls with recipes passed down three generations."

---

### Exercise 2: Add the Missing Human

**Start:** "Traditional cooking methods are demonstrated during the tour."

**Your turn:** Who's cooking? What's their name? How long have they been doing this?

**Sample answer:** "Watch Pak Hassan make roti canai the way his father taught him in 1975. Every flip matters."

---

### Exercise 3: Show, Don't Claim

**Start:** "Experience authentic, premium Malaysian cuisine at the best local establishments."

**Your turn:** What makes it authentic? What specific dishes? Who runs the place?

**Sample answer:** "Eat nasi lemak where taxi drivers queue at 6am. The place with no sign, just 40 years of the same family recipe."

## Common Mistakes to Watch For

### Mistake 1: Being Too Polite
❌ "We would be delighted to welcome you on our tour."
✅ "Come eat with us."

### Mistake 2: Over-Explaining
❌ "Our tours are designed to provide participants with comprehensive cultural context while sampling traditional dishes prepared by local vendors using time-honored methods."
✅ "You'll taste dishes vendors have made the same way for 40 years, and we'll tell you why it matters."

### Mistake 3: Hiding Behind "We"
❌ "We offer customized experiences."
✅ "Tell us what you're dreaming of. We'll make it happen." (Names the guide if possible: "Tell Zainal what you're dreaming of.")

### Mistake 4: No Sensory Details
❌ "Enjoy delicious local food."
✅ "Crispy on the outside, fluffy inside. Still hot from the wok."

### Mistake 5: Generic Social Proof
❌ "Highly rated by travelers."
✅ "TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2023. 15,000 travelers fed since 2011."

## Final Checklist Before Publishing

Read your copy and check:

- [ ] No em-dashes (use commas or split sentences)
- [ ] No "We're not X, we're Y" patterns
- [ ] No AI transitions (However, Moreover, etc.)
- [ ] No buzzwords (premium, authentic, world-class)
- [ ] No corporate hedging ("It's worth noting...")
- [ ] Includes real people (names, ages, years)
- [ ] Includes sensory details (crispy, aromatic, spicy)
- [ ] Sounds conversational when read aloud
- [ ] Shows, doesn't claim
- [ ] Could NOT be written by any other food tour company

If you pass all checks, you're writing in Simply Enak's voice.
