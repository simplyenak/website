### PILLAR 2: Maximize Review Quality & Strategic Volume

**Goal:** NOT to match 2,078 reviews
**Goal:** Get 100-150 reviews/year consistently (manageable, sustainable)

**Why This Works:**
1. **Review Recency** matters more than total count
   - 10 reviews in last month > 100 reviews from 2 years ago
   - TripAdvisor algorithm prioritizes recent activity

2. **Review Quality** drives conversions
   - One detailed story beats 10 generic "great tour!" reviews
   - Your reviews mention guides by name, specific vendors, emotional connections

3. **Review Response Rate** signals credibility
   - Respond to 100% of reviews within 24 hours
   - Chef's Tour likely can't keep up with 2,078 reviews

**Tactical Implementation:**

**Month 1-3: Review Quality Boost**
- Train guides to create "review-worthy moments"
- Identify 3-5 peak moments per tour to mention in post-tour email
- Example: "Remember Aunty Lim's story about her grandmother's laksa recipe?"

**Sustainable Review Generation:**
- Automated email sequence (Day 1, 3, 7)
- In-tour QR code + verbal ask
- Target: 50% review rate (vs industry standard 10-20%)
- If you do 200 tours/year → 100 reviews/year
- In 3 years: 600 total reviews (still credible, manageable)

- Competitive advantage: Guide expertise compounds over years
