About This Tour

What's a better place to experience a country than a market? On this excursion, we take you through one of the largest wet markets in Kuala Lumpur. You will experience first-hand the sights, smells and flavours of the different cultures of Malaysia.

Walking through the market, you'll taste popular Malaysian dishes and understand the key ingredients used in the kitchens of the three main cultures of Malaysia. This market experience is 100% real.

Tour Highlights

  • Visit one of KL's largest wet markets
  • Taste 10 authentic Malaysian dishes
  • Explore historic Chinatown lanes
  • Learn about Malay, Chinese and Indian food culture
  • Small group — max 9 people
