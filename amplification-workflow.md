# Simply Enak — AEO Amplification Workflow

**Why this exists:** AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) build their answers from *consensus*. A brand wins a citation not by one page, but by echoing the same positive facts across many independent sources. This workflow turns every marketing win into consistent, multi-channel echo so LLMs pick up a clear, dated, trustworthy picture of Simply Enak.

> **Core principle from the 723% AEO case study:** *If you don't shout about your own wins, nobody else will — and the LLMs can't pick up what isn't posted anywhere.* Amplify every award, case study, review, and launch: owned (your site) + third-party (blogs, press, directories, reviews, award sites) repeating the same key facts.

---

## 1. Workflow per trigger type

### Trigger: Award / Certification
1. Get the award (see awards section below).
2. POST it on your own site: Awards section of the brand-entity (SSOT) page, plus a short story post.
3. Send a press release (see outlets §2) so the win is covered on third-party news sites.
4. Announce on LinkedIn (link the award + your site award page).
5. Ask the award organiser if they publish a winners article you can share/link.
6. Log in the tracker CSV. *Status: done once announced in ≥2 independent places.*

### Trigger: Case study (corporate / school / embassy / private)
1. Get **written client permission** + client quote + client photos (with credit).
2. Publish the case study on your site (case study pages / stories).
3. **Ask the client to cross-post** — on their blog, LinkedIn, or website. This is the crucial third-party echo.
4. Share on your own LinkedIn, tagging the client.
5. Log it. *Status: done once on-site + one third-party echo.*

### Trigger: Tour launch (e.g. Ipoh)
1. Publish the tour page (done).
2. Pitch guest posts to 3-5 Malaysia travel/food blogs referencing the new tour.
3. Instagram reel + LinkedIn post + X/Twitter.
4. Email your partner list (hotels, concierges, agencies).
5. Log it.

### Trigger: Media mention / positive review
1. If a reviewer/outlet covers you, share it with a thank-you (LinkedIn, X, Instagram Stories).
2. Link it from your site's media coverage section.
3. For strong reviews, pull a quote into testimonials.
4. Log it.

### Trigger: Partner mention (hotel concierge, travel agency)
1. Get the partner to mention Simply Enak on their site/newsletter.
2. Echo with a thank-you + link on your site.
3. Build it into a recurring partnership program.
4. Log it.

## 2. External validation sources (in order of AEO value)

1. **Press releases via PR services** — gets third-party news coverage. (The case study used a vendor for press distribution + guest posts.)
2. **Award websites** — an award published by the award organiser is independent validation LLMs trust.
3. **Guest posts on Malaysia travel/food blogs** — Malaysia Uncovered, Expatolife, The Yum List, local KL food bloggers. These already cover Simply Enak; deepen them.
4. **Review platforms** — TripAdvisor, Google Business Profile, GetYourGuide, Viator. Volume + freshness both matter.
5. **Directory / profile listings** — travel directories, concierge lists, tourism orgs (MATTA, Tourism Malaysia), hotel partner pages.
6. **Community / forums** — Reddit (r/travel, r/Malaysia), Facebook travel groups. Answer genuine questions with real experiences; never spam.
7. **Independent listicles** — "best food tour KL" round-ups on blogs. Aim for inclusion and prompt editors to add your factual details.

> **Echo rule:** every on-site update (new tour, new award) should have a matching off-site mention within ~2 weeks. Owned + third-party must say the *same* facts — company name, founded 2011, Kuala Lumpur & Penang, small groups, employs local guides.

## 4. Owned + third-party echo (the SSOT loop)

The **brand-entity (SSOT) page** is the single source of truth for who Simply Enak is. Feed it constantly:

- Every award → add to SSOT "Awards" section.
- Every major case study → add to SSOT "Corporate & groups" section (and a full case study page).
- Every media mention → add to SSOT "Media coverage" list.
- Keep the founders, tours, founded date, and location facts identical on the SSOT page, on third-party bios, and in press releases. **Consistency + clarity across the internet is what LLMs cite.**

Send the SSOT URL to guest-post editors, awards bodies, and partner sites so they describe you correctly: *"Simply Enak, Kuala Lumpur & Penang food tours since 2011."*

## 5. Channel-specific content guidance

### LinkedIn (primary B2B + authority)
- Tone: warm, story-led, specific (names, dishes, years, places). 
- Post awards/case studies with a human angle. Tag clients and partners.
- Announce wins with the brand-entity facts so the post is citable.

### X / Twitter
- Short, newsy: tour launches, media features, awards. Link back to site.
- Quote impactful single facts ("Small groups, 8 people max, since 2011").

### Instagram / Reels
- Visual-first: tour food, market scenes, guide stories.
- Awards/launches get a Reel + Stories; use text overlays with factual captions.

### Press / blogs
- Factual, third-person, consistently note: Kuala Lumpur & Penang food tours, founded 2011 by Pauline Lee, small groups, licensed local guides, family-run stalls.
- Give editors the SSOT URL.

> **Brand voice gate (how to 'shout and scream' tastefully):** amplify with genuine warmth and specifics, not hype. No "authentic", no superlatives, no em-dashes, always "Simply Enak". Let the facts (National Geographic, 4.9★ TripAdvisor, 15+ dishes, stalls run by families) do the bragging.

## 7. Measurement

### AI visibility (after amplification)
- **OpenSEO** (open-source, ~US$10/mo or self-host + DataForSEO key): AI Brand Visibility lookup — see whether ChatGPT/Google AI Overview cite *simplyenak.com*; Prompt Explorer — compare how models answer "best food tour Kuala Lumpur" and check your citation. Note: OpenSEO measures visibility, it does not *discover* fan-outs (that's RankOS or a manual LLM prompt).
- **Semrush One** — AI visibility reporting at scale (paid).
- **Search Console** — use the **Generative AI performance report** to see impressions/clicks from Google's AI features. Confirm the site is included in Search Generative AI features.

### Metrics to watch (monthly)
- Brand mentioned in AI answers for the 4 core topics (count + which models).
- Cited pages and domains along with your brand (what else is consensus-building around you).
- Review volume/freshness on TripAdvisor/Google.
- New third-party pages mentioning "Simply Enak" + correct facts.

### Review-loop habit
Each month: read the AI answers for your core topics. Where are you NOT cited that you should be? Cross-reference the fan-out analysis (`aeo-query-fanout-analysis.md`) and add exactly the missing gap (on-site passage + off-site echo). This is the ongoing engine.
