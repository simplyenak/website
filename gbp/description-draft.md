Simply Enak runs small-group Malaysian food tours in Kuala Lumpur since 2011. We walk you through neighbourhoods where locals actually eat: family-run stalls in Chinatown, hawker legends of Kampung Baru, and late-night gems in Pudu. Expect 10 to 15 tastings, stories behind every recipe, and a guide who grew up with the food. Tours run daily, morning and evening, in English.
