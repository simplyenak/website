### Post 4 (Fri) — Traveler Tip
**Photo:** Hawker centre seating / ordering scene
```
How to order at a hawker centre like a local:

1. Find a table first, leave your tissue pack or bag on it (yes, that's the signal)
2. Order dish by dish at each stall - you pay each stall separately
3. Say "kurang manis" for less sweet, "pedas" if you want it spicy
4. Stallholders bring food to your table, no number buzzers needed

Put it to use on a KL food tour with us - we'll show you every step.

Book: simplyenak.com/tours
```
