### Payment Rules — Read This First

This is a mixed-payment tour. Getting it wrong means awkward moments with the customer. Memorise this:

| Symbol | Meaning | Your Action |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **SE pays** | Simply Enak covers the cost | You settle the bill, keep receipt, claim back from SE |
| **Customer pays** | Wanitha / the group pays | DO NOT offer to pay. Let Wanitha handle it. You may need to help coordinate (e.g. reservations) but the bill goes to them |
| **SE books, Customer pays** | SE arranges the booking, but food/drink costs are on Wanitha | Confirm the reservation; when the bill comes, direct it to Wanitha |

**Golden rule:** If in doubt, ask Wanitha before the bill arrives. Never guess.

## Tips for a Great Tour

1. **Carry a small float** — RM 300–500 in small notes for SE-covered food stops
2. **Collect receipts for EVERYTHING** SE pays for — photo the receipt immediately as backup
3. **Daily briefing** — each evening, tell the group (or Wanitha) the next day's plan
4. **Be clear about payments** — if there's any doubt who pays, ask Wanitha privately BEFORE arriving at the venue
5. **Weather watch** — Day 7 Balik Pulau (avoid midday heat), Day 10 boat (weather dependent)
6. **Cultural sensitivity** — Gurdwara (Day 2, cover head, remove shoes), Orang Asli (Day 3, be respectful guests)
7. **Have backup restaurants** — for every SE-arranged meal, know 1–2 alternatives nearby
8. **Keep the group hydrated** — always have water in the van, especially for outdoor days (Days 3, 5, 7, 9)
