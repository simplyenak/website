# B2B SEO Strategy — Culinary Travel Experts

## Current Reality

**Simply Enak (B2C):**
- 91K impressions, 1K clicks, 1.15% CTR
- 100% of traffic is B2C (food tours, durian, festivals)
- Top queries: "durian" (6K imp), "food tour penang" (206 imp)
- **Optimizing meta descriptions for price/rating will improve B2C CTR but won't help B2B**

**CTE (B2B):**
- 3 pages in sitemap (homepage, privacy, terms)
- Zero organic traffic (no GSC data available)
- No B2B-specific content
- No search visibility

## Strategic Problem

You can't optimize CTE for B2B without **B2B content**. Right now there's nothing to rank.

**Questions to answer:**

1. **What are travel agents searching for?**
   - "Malaysia food tour operator"
   - "DMC Malaysia culinary"
   - "Malaysian food experience partner"
   - We don't know because CTE has no content to capture these terms

2. **What content would rank?**
   - Destination guides for agents (not tourists)
   - Operational docs (licensing, insurance, capacity)
   - Sample itineraries for trade
   - Case studies from partner agents

3. **Is B2B SEO worth the investment?**
   - CTE is a "category of one" — no competitors
   - But also: zero search demand (nobody searches for what doesn't exist)
   - B2B sales come from relationships, not SEO

## Recommended Actions

### Phase 1: Foundation (Do This First)
1. **Add GSC verification** — so we can see what (if anything) ranks
2. **Build 5-10 B2B pages:**
   - "For Travel Agents"
   - "Destination Operations Guide"
   - "Licensing & Insurance"
   - "Sample Itineraries"
   - "Partner Testimonials"
3. **Add B2B schema** — `ProfessionalService`, `Offer`, `BreadcrumbList`

### Phase 2: Content (If Phase 1 Shows Promise)
1. **Create B2B-focused content:**
   - "How to Sell Malaysian Food Tours" (for agents)
   - "Malaysia Food Travel: Agent's Guide"
   - Destination deep-dives (KL, Penang, Ipoh) from operator perspective
2. **Target B2B long-tail keywords:**
   - "Malaysia DMC for food tours"
   - "Malaysian culinary tour operator for travel agents"
   - "Private food tour operator Malaysia B2B"

### Phase 3: Scale (If B2B SEO Works)
1. **Malay-language B2B content** — capture local agents
2. **Partnership directory** — showcase agent partners
3. **Case studies** — agent success stories

## What NOT to Do
- Don't optimize Simply Enak homepage for B2B — it confuses users and dilutes B2C messaging
- Don't add B2B schema to Simply Enak — it's a B2C brand
- Don't chase B2B keywords on Simply Enak — they're not there

## Bottom Line
CTE needs **content first, SEO second**. Without B2B-specific pages, there's nothing to optimize. Start with 5 foundational pages, verify GSC, then measure.
