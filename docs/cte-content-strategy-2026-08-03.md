# CTE Content Strategy — B2B Pages

## The Problem
- Zero search volume for B2B culinary terms
- Agents don't search for "DMC Malaysia food" — they ask for recommendations
- Pages are skeletons, not usable resources
- CTAs lead to dead ends

## The Goal
- Convert visitors who find you through LinkedIn, referrals, or direct search
- Give agents confidence you can deliver
- Make the partnership decision easy

---

## Page-by-Page Content Plan

### 1. /for-agents (Partner Page)
**Purpose:** Answer "Why partner with you?" and "How does it work?"

**Sections to write:**
- **Header:** "Sell Malaysia's best food experiences without the operational headache"
- **The Problem:** Agents want to offer authentic food tours but can't verify quality, handle logistics, or guarantee consistency
- **The Solution:** We handle operations; you handle relationships
- **Commission Structure:** Be transparent — 20-25% on tours, 15% on private groups
- **What Agents Get:**
  - Dedicated account manager
  - Pre-negotiated rates (no surprise costs)
  - Co-branded marketing materials
  - Fast booking turnaround (24 hours)
  - 24/7 guest support in Malaysia
- **How to Start:** 3-step process (Register → Access Trade Kit → Book First Tour)
- **Testimonials:** Quote from existing partners (even if just 2-3)

**Target length:** 800-1000 words

---

### 2. /dmc-services (Capabilities Page)
**Purpose:** Show you can handle any group size or request

**Sections to write:**
- **Header:** "Malaysia's culinary DMC — licensed, insured, experienced"
- **Group Types:**
  - FIT (2-8 travelers): Private guides, custom itineraries
  - Small Groups (8-20): Join-in tours, team building
  - Large Groups (20-200): Corporate events, FAM trips
- **What's Included:**
  - Licensed guides (English, Malay, Mandarin, Cantonese)
  - All food and drinks
  - Transportation (air-con vehicles)
  - Hotel pickup/drop-off
  - Dietary accommodation (halal, vegetarian, vegan, allergies)
- **Destinations:** KL, Penang, Ipoh, Melaka, Cameron Highlands, Johor Bahru
- **Licensing:** MOTAC registered, fully insured, public liability coverage
- **Case Study:** "Recently hosted a 45-person food industry delegation from Europe..."

**Target length:** 600-800 words

---

**Purpose:** Give agents the tools to sell

**Fix the broken links:**

**Content to create:**

  - Top 5 food experiences
  - Best times to visit
  - Pricing guide
  - Agent talking points
- **Sample Itineraries:**

  - "Penang Heritage Food Trail"

- **Marketing Kit:**
  - High-res photos (10-15)
  - Pre-written social media posts

- **Commission Calculator:** Simple spreadsheet or tool

**Target length:** 500 words + downloadable assets

---

### 4. /sustainability (Impact Page)
**Purpose:** Give agents a story to tell their clients

**Sections to write:**
- **Header:** "Every booking supports a family, preserves a tradition"
- **Our Model:**
  - 50+ family-run vendor partnerships
  - 14 years of relationships
  - Direct payments (no middlemen)
- **Impact Stories:**
  - "The Chen family has been making char siew bao for 60 years. Your clients taste what their grandmother made."
  - "Pak Hassan's fishing boat has operated the same way since the 1970s. We bring 12 guests twice a week."
- **Sustainability Practices:**
  - Sourcing from local producers
  - Supporting heritage recipes
  - Fair pricing for vendors
- **What Agents Can Say:**
  - Pre-written talking points for clients
  - "This isn't a tour — it's a contribution to keeping Malaysian food culture alive"

**Target length:** 600-800 words

---

### 5. /blog (Agent Insights)
**Purpose:** Position you as the expert agents trust

**First 5 articles to write:**

1. **"Why Your Clients Will Remember This More Than Any Museum Tour"**
   - The psychology of food memories
   - Why authentic experiences convert to referrals
   - Case study: client who brought 3 friends back

2. **"How to Sell Malaysian Food Tours to Skeptical Clients"**
   - Common objections ("Will they like the food?")
   - How to position the experience
   - Scripts for agent conversations

3. **"Durian Season: What Agents Need to Know (And What to Tell Clients)"**
   - When it happens (June-September)
   - How to manage expectations
   - Which tours to offer

4. **"Halal, Vegetarian, Vegan: Handling Dietary Requirements in Malaysia"**
   - What each term means in Malaysian context
   - How to communicate with guests
   - What we handle for you

5. **"The Difference Between a Food Tour and a Cooking Class (And When to Recommend Each)"**
   - Pros/cons of each format
   - Who each is for
   - How to bundle them

**Target length:** 1,000-1,500 words per article

---

## What to Skip (For Now)
- Homepage redesign — current one works
- Multi-language versions — focus on English first
- Complex booking system — keep it simple (contact form → email → quote)

## Priority Order
1. **for-agents** — Most important, highest conversion potential
2. **dmc-services** — Second priority, builds credibility
3. **sustainability** — Differentiator, gives agents talking points
4. **fam-trip-resources** — Needs actual assets first
5. **blog** — Long-term play, start with 2-3 articles

## Tone Guidelines
- **Not:** "We are the best food tour operator in Malaysia"
- **Is:** "Here's what you need to know to sell this confidently"
- **Not:** Promotional, salesy language
- **Is:** Practical, operational, helpful

Think of it as writing for a busy travel agent who needs to make a decision in 5 minutes.
