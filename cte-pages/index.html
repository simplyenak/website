Who We Are

How we treat complex requests.

We have been running food experiences in Malaysia since 2010. Market tours, heritage walks, cooking classes. Over time, hotels and travel agents started asking us to run things for their guests. Not because they wanted to outsource, but because their guests wanted things standard suppliers couldn't deliver. A halal street food tour that also covered history. A gluten-free guest who wanted to cook Nyonya food. A corporate group of 40 with dietary notes covering four religions.

We could say yes because we know the stalls, the kitchens, the guides. We know which requests are possible and which ones need a Plan B. Fifteen years of that, and you build a pretty deep Rolodex. Culinary Travel Experts is the trade-facing side of that Rolodex.
