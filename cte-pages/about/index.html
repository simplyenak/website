About Culinary Travel Experts

We started by running food tours. Then hotels, DMCs, and food organisers kept asking us to run things for their guests. Not because they wanted to outsource. Because their guests wanted things standard suppliers couldn't deliver.

The Story

From food tours to full trip design.

Simply Enak has been running food experiences in Kuala Lumpur and Penang since 2010. Walking food tours through George Town. Market visits in Chow Kit. Private eating trips for visitors who wanted more than a restaurant recommendation from their hotel concierge.

Over time, something happened we did not plan for. Hotels started calling. Concierge services wanted multi-day programmes for their guests. Food organisers asked if we could build full itineraries for groups of 12, then 20, then 40. Travel agents wanted to know if we could run a 5-day eating trip across peninsular Malaysia.

The trade came to us because nobody else was doing this with the level of specificity they needed. They wanted someone who could name the right stall at Pasar Borong Selayang, who knew which Kampung Baru nasi lemak vendor made their own sambal from scratch, who could explain the difference between a Nyonya kitchen and a Malay kampung kitchen in terms a guest would remember.

Culinary Travel Experts was created to give that demand a dedicated channel. Same team. Same knowledge. Structured for trade partners.

How We Work

Built on knowledge, not fam trips.

15 years of eating the same places

Thousands of meals at hundreds of stalls, restaurants, markets, and home kitchens. We know the vendors by name, the stories behind the buildings, and when a stall's quality drops because the second generation took over.

Specific, not general

We do not send people to "a banana leaf restaurant." We send them to the one in Brickfields where the rasam has the right balance of tamarind and pepper, and we teach them how to eat with their hands properly. The specificity is the product.

Tested by us first

Every stop in our programme has been eaten, walked, and vetted by our founders. If a vendor's quality slips, we know. If a new place opens that is better than the old standby, we switch. We do not pull itineraries from a database.
