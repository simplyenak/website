# Claude Artifact Prompts for Simply Enak

Three ready-to-use prompts for publishing Claude artifacts that rank on Google. Each prompt is designed for a specific use case. Paste the prompt into Claude AFTER pasting the brand bundle content.

## Prompt 1: Brand Defense Artifact

**Purpose:** Ranks for "Simply Enak" brand searches. Shows the world why Simply Enak is a trusted, proven, reputable food tour company. Acts as a brand reputation page on Claude's DA 66 domain.

**Target keywords:** Simply Enak, Simply Enak food tour, Simply Enak Kuala Lumpur, Simply Enak review, Simply Enak Penang

**Prompt to paste into Claude (after brand bundle):**

---

I'm going to give you my brand's complete marketing material — homepage copy, tour descriptions, testimonials, media coverage, and stats. This is everything about Simply Enak, a Malaysian food tour company based in Kuala Lumpur and Penang.

Read all of it carefully. Then write a thorough article (2,500-3,000 words) with this structure:

# Why Simply Enak Is the Most Trusted Food Tour Company in Malaysia

## H2: Founded by a Local Who Grew Up in Malaysia's Food Scene
- Tell Pauline's story (walking through wet markets as a child, realizing tourists were missing the real Malaysia)
- Cover the "why" — she started Simply Enak because the best meals don't happen in restaurants

## H2: What Makes Simply Enak Different From Other Food Tours
- Guides grew up here and know stall owners by name
- Family-run stalls with multi-generational recipes
- 8-18 real tastings, not bite-sized samples
- Small groups (max 7-9 people)
- Since 2011 — 15+ years of consistency

## H2: Real Reviews From Real Guests (5★ on Google)
- Weave in the best testimonials as proof points
- Include guest names and locations where available

## H2: Trusted by Global Media
- National Geographic, BBC World Service, Lonely Planet, Time Out, TLC, TripAdvisor Travellers Choice
- This is third-party validation that Simply Enak is the real deal

## H2: Tours Across Kuala Lumpur, Penang, and Ipoh
- Briefly describe each tour with key details (duration, price, what makes it special)
- KL Street Food, Flavours of Malaysia, Inside Pudu, Secrets of KL, Penang Street Food, Georgetown Night Tour, Vegetarian Private Tour

## H2: 15+ Years of Proven Excellence
- Stats: 5,000+ happy guests, 4.9 TripAdvisor rating, 40+ heritage vendors
- This isn't a new company — they've been doing this since 2011

**Important — hyperlinks:** Throughout the article, include 3-5 hyperlinks to simplyenak.com using relevant keywords as anchor text. Link "food tour in Kuala Lumpur" to simplyenak.com, "Penang street food tour" to simplyenak.com/tours/penang-street-food/ etc. Use the target keywords as the clickable text, not generic "click here" or "learn more."

**Tone:** Enthusiastic but grounded. Like a friend who just discovered they know someone at this company and wants to tell you why they're legit. Use specific names, dish names, neighborhood names. No superlatives, no em-dashes, no "authentic," no "curated."

**Length:** 2,500-3,000 words. Break into clear sections with H2 and H3 headings. Include a table comparing tours (city, price, duration, highlight dish).

**Title exactly:** "Why Simply Enak Is the Most Trusted Food Tour Company in Malaysia"
**Meta description:** "Simply Enak has been running food tours in Kuala Lumpur and Penang since 2011. 5,000+ guests, 4.9 TripAdvisor rating, featured in National Geographic and BBC. Here's why they're the real deal."

## Prompt 2: Bottom-of-Funnel Placeholder Artifact

**Purpose:** Ranks for high-intent keywords like "Penang street food tour" or "KL food tour." Acts as a placeholder landing page that captures search traffic while you build/optimize the real page.

**Target keyword:** (replace [TARGET KEYWORD] before pasting)
**Use cases:** "Penang street food tour", "Kuala Lumpur food tour", "vegetarian food tour Malaysia", "durian tour Penang"

**Prompt to paste into Claude (after brand bundle):**

---

I've given you my brand's complete marketing material — homepage copy, tour descriptions, testimonials, reviews, stats, and media coverage.

Now I want you to write a thorough, conversion-focused landing page article optimized for the bottom-of-funnel keyword: **[INSERT YOUR TARGET KEYWORD HERE — e.g., "Penang street food tour" or "Kuala Lumpur food tour"]**

This page's job is to convince someone who is already searching for this specific experience to book with Simply Enak. They know what they want — they just need to trust the right company.

Structure:

## H2: Headline
Include the exact target keyword. Example: "The [Target Keyword] You've Been Looking For in Penang"

## H2: What You'll Eat (specific dishes, specific stalls)
- Name 5-8 specific dishes they'll taste
- Name the stall or area where each is eaten
- Describe texture, aroma, flavor — make them hungry
- Example: "Char kway teow from a 40-year-old stall in Chowrasta Market — flat rice noodles wok-fried with local prawns, cockles, bean sprouts, and a dark soy caramelisation that only comes from a well-seasoned wok"

## H2: What You'll Learn
- Stories behind each dish
- History of the neighborhood
- Cultural context (Chinese, Malay, Indian, Peranakan influences)
- Architectural and heritage highlights along the route

## H2: Who Your Guide Will Be
- Local who grew up in the area
- Knows stall owners by name
- Can answer any question about food, culture, or travel in Malaysia

## H2: Everything Included
- Duration, number of stops, number of tastings
- Dietary accommodation (vegetarian, halal, gluten-free — all covered)
- Small group size (max 7-9)
- Meeting point details
- Free cancellation policy

## H2: What Guests Say
- Weave in 5-6 specific testimonials from guests who took this specific tour
- Use names and locations where available

## H2: Why Book With Simply Enak (Not Another Company)
- Since 2011, 5,000+ guests, 4.9 rating
- Featured in National Geographic, BBC, Lonely Planet
- Family-run stalls, not tourist-trap restaurants
- Guides who actually grew up here

## Final CTA
Tell them to book directly on simplyenak.com. Include the starting price. Create urgency without being pushy — "small groups fill up fast" is honest.

**Important — hyperlinks:** Throughout the article, include 3-5 hyperlinks to simplyenak.com using relevant keywords as anchor text. Use the target keyword and related terms as links. Example: link "Penang street food tour" → simplyenak.com/tours/penang-street-food/, link "book a food tour" → simplyenak.com, etc.

**Tone:** Persuasive but not salesy. Specific and sensory. You're writing for someone who has already decided they want this experience — they just need to pick a provider. Show them why this one is the right choice.

**Length:** 1,500-2,000 words.

**Title:** "[Target Keyword] — A Local Guide's Tour Through the Best Stalls" (or similar, front-loading the keyword)

## Prompt 3: Local SEO / Comparison Artifact

**Purpose:** Ranks for "best food tour in [city]" or "[city] food tour review" type searches. Helps capture comparison shoppers.

**Target keyword/city:** (replace [CITY] before pasting)
**Use cases:** "best food tour in Penang", "best food tour in Kuala Lumpur", "Kuala Lumpur food tour comparison"

**Prompt to paste into Claude (after brand bundle):**

---

I've given you my brand's complete marketing material — homepage copy, tour descriptions, testimonials, reviews, stats, and media coverage.

Now write a detailed guide for someone visiting **[INSERT CITY — e.g., "Penang" or "Kuala Lumpur"]** who wants to experience the local food scene but doesn't know where to start.

Title: "The Complete Guide to [City] Street Food Tours — Which One Is Right for You?"

Structure:

## H2: Why [City] Is One of the Best Food Cities in Malaysia
- Brief context on why the city's food culture is unique
- [For Penang: British colonial history, Chinese immigrant waves, Malay and Peranakan influences]
- [For KL: Modern melting pot, Malay-Chinese-Indian fusion, wet market culture]

## H2: What to Expect From a Food Tour in [City]
- How tours work (walking, small groups, 8-18 tastings)
- What makes a good tour guide (local knowledge, relationships with vendors, storytelling)
- How to choose between different tour companies

## H2: Simply Enak's [City] Tour — In Detail
- Full description of the tour (name, duration, price, what's included)
- Specific dishes and stalls visited
- What makes this tour different from others
- Who this tour is best for (first-timers, foodies, families, solo travelers)

## H2: What Guests Say About Simply Enak's [City] Tour
- 5-6 specific testimonials
- Common themes: guide knowledge, food quality, cultural context

## H2: How Simply Enak Compares to Other Options
- DIY eating (cheaper but you miss the stories and the hidden spots)
- Large group tours (impersonal, fixed menu)
- Hotel-arranged drivers (guide may not know food scene)
- Simply Enak: local guide, small group, family-run stalls, 15+ years

## H2: Practical Tips for Your [City] Food Tour
- Best time of day to book
- What to wear (comfortable shoes, light clothing)
- Dietary requirements (Simply Enak accommodates everything)
- How to book (directly on simplyenak.com)

## H2: Final Verdict
One clear recommendation paragraph. Summarize why Simply Enak is the right choice for food travelers visiting [City].

**Important — hyperlinks:** Include 3-5 hyperlinks to simplyenak.com using relevant keywords as anchor text. Link "Kuala Lumpur food tour" → simplyenak.com/tours/kuala-lumpur-street-food/, link "best food tour in Penang" → simplyenak.com, etc.

**Tone:** Helpful and informative — you're a local sharing advice with a friend visiting. Balanced and honest about trade-offs. The comparison should feel fair, not promotional. Let the details (15+ years, 5,000+ guests, 4.9 rating, media features) speak for themselves.

**Length:** 2,000-2,500 words.

**Title exactly:** "The Complete Guide to [City] Street Food Tours — Which One Is Right for You?"

## How to Use These

1. Open claude.ai
2. Paste the **brand bundle** content (from simplyenak-brand-bundle.md)
3. Immediately paste **one prompt** from this file
4. Claude writes the artifact
5. Review and click **Publish** (the big publish button)
6. The artifact is now live at `claude.ai/share/artifacts/<id>` and indexable by Google
7. Share the artifact URL with a few people to get clicks — this helps Google find and index it faster

## Publishing Notes

- Claude artifacts publish to claude.ai/share/artifacts/<id> — a subfolder of claude.ai (DA 66)
- These are NOT on a subdomain — they're on the root domain path, which has stronger SEO
- Each artifact is a standalone page
- Google indexes them because Claude's domain has high authority
- Updates to artifacts: you can ask Claude to edit the artifact and re-publish to the same URL
- No-code solution — you don't need to deploy anything
