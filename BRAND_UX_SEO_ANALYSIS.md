### "The Passionate Friend" Archetype Implementation

#### ✅ What's Working:

1. **Show, Don't Tell** - Excellent implementation:
   - ❌ Never says "we're authentic"
   - ✅ Says "Aunty Lim's been making this laksa for 40 years"
   - ❌ Never says "premium experience"
   - ✅ Says "TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2023"
   - ❌ Never says "we're your friends"
   - ✅ Demonstrates friendship through warmth, specific recommendations, personal stories

2. **Personal Stories Over Features**:
   - Vegetarian page: "Her grandmother arrived from Tamil Nadu with just cooking pots"
   - KL page: "Families have spent decades perfecting spice blends"
   - NO generic "visit 8 establishments" language

3. **Specific Names & Details**:
   - "Aunty Maya's Brickfields Legacy" not "traditional restaurant"
   - "Master Chen's Temple Kitchen" not "Buddhist cooking experience"
   - "Chef Raj's Urban Innovation Lab" not "modern plant-based venue"

#### ⚠️ Areas for Improvement:

1. **Conversion Language**:
   - Current: "Book Now" buttons
   - Better (More Friend-like): "Come eat with us" / "Join our next tour" / "Let's explore together"
   - Buttons are functional but could be warmer

2. **About Page** (Not yet reviewed):
   - Should tell Simply Enak's founding story
   - Should introduce the team as real people
   - Should explain WHY you started (passion, not profit)

3. **Thank You Pages** (Not yet reviewed):
   - Critical touchpoint to reinforce friendship
   - Should feel like "Can't wait to see you!" not "Transaction complete"
