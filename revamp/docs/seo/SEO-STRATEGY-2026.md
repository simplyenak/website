| **Fluency** | Well-structured, scannable content | +15-30% |

### Platform-Specific Tactics

**ChatGPT Optimization:**
- Update content every 30 days (freshness signal)
- Build backlinks from travel authority sites
- Match ChatGPT's response format (lists, clear answers)

**Perplexity Optimization:**
- FAQ schema on every page
- Allow PerplexityBot in robots.txt ✅ (already allowed)
- Create PDF downloads (tour itineraries, food guides)

**Google AI Overview:**
- E-E-A-T signals: author bios, vendor credentials
- Structured data (Schema.org)
- Authoritative citations (Tourism Malaysia, Michelin Guide)

**Claude Optimization:**
- Ensure Brave Search indexing
- High factual density (specific dates, names, dishes)
- Clear structure (H1-H3 hierarchy)
