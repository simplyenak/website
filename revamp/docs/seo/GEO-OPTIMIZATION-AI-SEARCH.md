### Method 4: Authoritative Tone (+25% AI Visibility)

**What it means:** Confident, expert language signals trustworthiness.

**Implementation:**

```markdown
## Example: Tour Description

### BEFORE (Weak, Uncertain)
"We think you'll enjoy our food tour. It might include some street food."

### AFTER (Authoritative)
"Simply Enak's Kuala Lumpur Food Tour is the definitive authentic Malaysian street food experience. You will visit 6 generational vendors, taste 10-12 dishes prepared using recipes unchanged since the 1970s-80s, and walk through neighborhoods where locals actually eat. Your guide, born and raised in Kuala Lumpur, provides cultural context for every dish. This is not a tourist food tour — this is how Malaysians actually experience their own cuisine."
```

**Tone Guidelines:**
- ✅ Use definitive statements: "You will", "This is", "We guarantee"
- ✅ Avoid hedging: "might", "could", "possibly", "we think"
- ✅ State expertise: "Our guides are trained in...", "We've operated since..."
- ✅ Make specific claims: "40+ year vendors", "not tourist menus"

- Use H2/H3 headers for every section

- Bold key terms

### Method 8: Fluency Optimization (+15-30% AI Visibility)

**What it means:** Smooth, readable content is more likely to be cited.

**Fluency Checklist:**
- [ ] Vary sentence length (mix short and long)
- [ ] Use transition words: however, therefore, meanwhile, specifically
- [ ] Avoid repetition (use synonyms)
- [ ] Active voice over passive
- [ ] Clear pronoun references
- [ ] Logical paragraph flow

**Example:**

```markdown
### BEFORE (Choppy, Hard to Follow)
"We have food tours. The tours are in KL. KL is the capital. The food is good. Many people like it. You should book now."

### AFTER (Fluent, Flowing)
"Simply Enak operates authentic food tours throughout Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia's vibrant capital city. Our tours showcase the city's diverse culinary landscape — from Chinatown's century-old hawker stalls to Little India's aromatic roti canai shops. With over 2,800 guests served and a perfect 4.9 rating, our tours have become the preferred choice for travelers seeking genuine Malaysian food experiences. Ready to taste the real Kuala Lumpur? Book your tour today."
```
