### Review Acquisition Plan

**Goal:** 500+ reviews by December 2026 (12 months)

**Monthly Target:** 35-40 new reviews/month

**Tactics:**
1. **Pre-tour email** — Set expectations, build excitement
2. **Post-tour email (Day 1)** — "How was your experience?" + review link
3. **WhatsApp reminder (Day 2)** — High open rate, personal touch
4. **In-tour request** — Guide mentions at end: "If you enjoyed today..."
5. **Incentive** — RM 30 discount on next tour for review with photos
6. **TripAdvisor claim** — List business, add review link to booking confirmation

**Review Script for Guides:**
```
"Hey everyone, if you enjoyed today's experience, we'd love a review on TripAdvisor or Google.
It helps us continue supporting our vendor partners — families who've been serving the same
recipes for 40+ years. I'll send you a link — takes 2 minutes, means the world to us!"
```
