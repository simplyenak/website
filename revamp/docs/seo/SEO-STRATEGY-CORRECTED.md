## 🎭 Brand Archetype: The Passionate Friend

### How to Communicate (From simply_enak_brand_guidelines.md)

**Voice:** Warm, conversational, enthusiastic but authentic

**DO:**
- Use short punchy sentences mixed with longer descriptive ones
- Ask questions: "Want to see what most tourists miss?"
- Use contractions: we'll, you'll, don't
- Share personal anecdotes: "Aunty Lim's been making this laksa for 40 years"
- Use sensory descriptions: "wok-charred noodles", "coconut-scented rice"

**DON'T:**
- Use tourism jargon: "authentic", "premium", "world-class", "best"
- Use corporate speak: "customer/client" → use "guest/friend"
- Use generic descriptors without specifics
- Use multiple exclamation points
- Use pressure language: "must-see", "can't-miss"

### The Three-Feeling Test

**Every piece of content must make your audience feel:**

1. **WELCOMED** — Like a friend, not a customer
2. **FASCINATED** — Curious about hidden Malaysia
3. **CONNECTED** — Linked to Malaysian heritage

**Decision Rule:** YES to all three → Create it. NO to any → Refine or don't publish.
