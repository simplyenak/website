# Simply Enak - Words to Avoid Guide
**The Passionate Friend Voice: What NOT to Say**

---

## Core Principle

**Friends don't talk like tourism brochures.**

When you catch yourself using these words, stop and ask:
- "Would I actually say this to a friend?"
- "Am I claiming or showing?"
- "Could any tour company say this?"

If yes to the last two, rewrite.

---

## FORBIDDEN WORDS & PHRASES

### Category 1: Tourism Industry Buzzwords

#### ❌ Adventure / Adventures
**Why forbidden:** Thrill-seeking, adrenaline-focused, individual achievement. Not warmth or connection.

**What friends say instead:**
- "Come eat with us"
- "Let's walk through my neighborhood"
- "Want to see where we go?"
- "Join us for food"

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Book Your Adventure"
- ✅ "Come Eat With Us"
- ❌ "Join Our Food Adventure"
- ✅ "Join Our Food Tour"

---

#### ❌ Journey
**Why forbidden:** Overused, vague, spiritual/self-discovery connotations. Too grand for eating food.

**What friends say instead:**
- Tour, walk, time together, visit
- "Let's go eat"
- "Walk with me"

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Embark on a culinary journey"
- ✅ "Come taste what we love"
- ❌ "Your Malaysia journey starts here"
- ✅ "Your Malaysia tour starts here"

**Exception:** "Journey" is okay when literal (physical travel):
- ✅ "4-hour journey through Georgetown" (if it's actually 4 hours of walking)

---

#### ❌ Discover / Discovery
**Why forbidden:** Explorer mindset, Columbus vibes, implies things are hidden for you to find. Condescending.

**What friends say instead:**
- See, meet, taste, learn, find
- "Let me show you"
- "Want to see?"

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Discover authentic Malaysia"
- ✅ "See the Malaysia tourists miss"
- ❌ "Discover hidden flavors"
- ✅ "Taste flavors you never knew existed"

---

#### ❌ Explore / Exploration
**Why forbidden:** Same as discover. Safari mindset, not friendship mindset.

**What friends say instead:**
- Walk through, visit, eat at, see
- "Let's go to..."
- "Walk through..."

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Explore KL's food scene"
- ✅ "Walk through KL's neighborhoods where we eat"
- ❌ "Explore by City"
- ✅ "Choose Your City" OR "Pick Your Location"

---

#### ❌ Experience (as a noun/verb when vague)
**Why forbidden:** Corporate wellness retreat language. Vague, overused, meaningless.

**What friends say instead:**
- Tour, walk, time with us, visit
- Specific verbs: eat, taste, meet, see, learn

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Book Your Experience"
- ✅ "Join This Tour"
- ❌ "Create Your Experience"
- ✅ "Plan Your Tour" OR "Let's Plan This"
- ❌ "Experience Malaysia"
- ✅ "Eat with us in Malaysia"

**Exception:** "Experience" is okay when specific and descriptive:
- ✅ "You'll experience the heat of the wok" (sensory, specific)
- ✅ "14+ years of experience" (noun, credential)

---

#### ❌ Immersive / Immersion
**Why forbidden:** VR headset language, corporate training speak. Pretentious.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Just describe what actually happens)
- "You'll eat, walk, talk, meet..."

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Immersive cultural experience"
- ✅ "You'll meet vendors, hear their stories, taste their recipes"
- ❌ "Immerse yourself in Malaysian culture"
- ✅ "Walk through neighborhoods. Meet families. Eat their food."

---

### Category 2: Fake Authenticity Claims

#### ❌ Authentic / Authenticity
**Why forbidden:** If you have to say it, it's not. Show it instead.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Show through specific details: names, ages, years, stories)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Authentic Malaysian food"
- ✅ "Aunty Lim's laksa, same recipe since 1982"
- ❌ "Authentic local experience"
- ✅ "Places we actually eat with our families"

---

#### ❌ Genuine
**Why forbidden:** Same as authentic. Defensive, claims instead of shows.

**What friends say instead:**
- Real (only if necessary), actual, or just describe it

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Genuine cultural connection"
- ✅ "You'll meet vendors who've become our friends over 14 years"
- ❌ "Genuine local guides"
- ✅ "Guides who grew up eating at these stalls"

---

#### ❌ Real / Really (when claiming authenticity)
**Why forbidden:** Defensive, implies there's a fake version

**What friends say instead:**
- (Just state the fact without "real")

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Real Malaysia"
- ✅ "Malaysia" OR "The Malaysia tourists miss"
- ❌ "Real local food"
- ✅ "Where locals eat" OR "Family-run stalls"

**Exception:** "Real" is okay when contrasting with something specific:
- ✅ "Real stories, not tourist performances"
- ✅ "These are real relationships, not transactions"

---

### Category 3: Luxury/Premium Language

#### ❌ Premium
**Why forbidden:** Hotel marketing speak. Implies expensive, exclusive, better than others.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Show quality through details: years, methods, ingredients)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Premium food tour"
- ✅ "Small groups (8 max), guides who grew up here, 14+ years experience"
- ❌ "Premium culinary experience"
- ✅ "Taste dishes from vendors our families have known for decades"

---

#### ❌ Luxury / Luxurious
**Why forbidden:** Hotels, spas, champagne. Not street food, not friendship.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Just describe what makes it special)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Luxury food tour"
- ✅ "Food tour with guides who know these vendors personally"

---

#### ❌ World-Class
**Why forbidden:** Corporate conference speak. Meaningless, unverifiable.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Use actual awards/recognition)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "World-class guides"
- ✅ "Licensed by Tourism Malaysia, TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2023"
- ❌ "World-class Malaysian cuisine"
- ✅ "UNESCO-recognized Georgetown food heritage"

---

#### ❌ Exclusive / Exclusively
**Why forbidden:** VIP lounge vibes, exclusionary, not welcoming.

**What friends say instead:**
- Only, just, specifically
- "We only visit..." = factual
- "Exclusive access" = pretentious

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Exclusive access to local vendors"
- ✅ "We visit family-run stalls our guides have known for years"
- ✅ "We only work with vendors who preserve traditional methods" (factual use of "only")

---

### Category 4: Pressure/Hype Language

#### ❌ Must-See / Can't-Miss / Don't-Miss
**Why forbidden:** FOMO manipulation, pressure tactics, not friendly.

**What friends say instead:**
- "Want to see...?"
- "You'll love..."
- "Worth checking out"

**Examples:**
- ❌ "A must-see food tour"
- ✅ "The food tour locals recommend to visiting friends"
- ❌ "Can't-miss Malaysian experience"
- ✅ "What we show our own friends when they visit"

---

#### ❌ Best / Top / #1 (when claiming, not proving)
**Why forbidden:** Unverifiable claims, every tour company says it.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Use actual proof: awards, rankings, reviews)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "The best food tour in KL"
- ✅ "TripAdvisor #1 Rated Food Tour in KL" (if true, with link)
- ❌ "Top Malaysian food experience"
- ✅ "1,250+ guests, 4.9 rating, Travellers' Choice Award"

**Exception:** "Best" is okay when personal opinion:
- ✅ "This is our favorite laksa spot in Georgetown"
- ✅ "Aunty Lim makes the best char kway teow we've ever tasted"

---

#### ❌ Amazing / Incredible / Unforgettable
**Why forbidden:** Generic, lazy, tells instead of shows. AI default adjectives.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Sensory details that make them imagine it)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Amazing flavors"
- ✅ "Tangy tamarind balanced with fiery sambal. Crispy roti fresh from the griddle."
- ❌ "Unforgettable experience"
- ✅ "People email us years later asking for Aunty Lim's address"
- ❌ "Incredible food tour"
- ✅ "8-10 dishes, 5 hours, stories you'll tell for years"

---

### Category 5: Corporate/Formal Language

#### ❌ Customer / Client
**Why forbidden:** Transactional, business-to-consumer, not friend-to-friend.

**What friends say instead:**
- Guest, friend, you, people who join us

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Our customers love..."
- ✅ "Our guests tell us..." OR "People who join us say..."
- ❌ "Customer service"
- ✅ "How we take care of you" OR just "service"

---

#### ❌ Establishment / Venue
**Why forbidden:** Health inspector language, formal, distant.

**What friends say instead:**
- Stall, shop, place, spot, restaurant (if it's actually a restaurant)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "We visit 8 authentic establishments"
- ✅ "We'll stop at 8 stalls our families have known for decades"
- ❌ "Local dining venues"
- ✅ "Places where locals eat"

---

#### ❌ Provide / Offering / Deliver
**Why forbidden:** Service provider speak, transactional.

**What friends say instead:**
- Show you, share, take you to, introduce you to

**Examples:**
- ❌ "We provide authentic food experiences"
- ✅ "We show you where we actually eat"
- ❌ "Offering cultural insights"
- ✅ "Sharing stories behind every dish"

---

#### ❌ Professional / Professionalism
**Why forbidden:** Corporate, stiff, implies others are amateur.

**What friends say instead:**
- Licensed, experienced, trained (when it's a fact)
- (Or just show competence through details)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Professional tour guides"
- ✅ "Licensed by Tourism Malaysia" OR "Guides who grew up in these neighborhoods"
- ❌ "Professional service"
- ✅ "We respond within 4 hours" (shows reliability without claiming it)

---

### Category 6: AI Speak Patterns

#### ❌ However / Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally
**Why forbidden:** Academic essay transitions, robotic, not conversational.

**What friends say instead:**
- And, but, plus, also, here's the thing

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Moreover, our tours include cultural context"
- ✅ "Plus, we share the cultural stories"
- ❌ "Furthermore, we accommodate dietary needs"
- ✅ "And yes, we handle dietary restrictions"

---

#### ❌ It's important to note / It's worth mentioning / One should consider
**Why forbidden:** Corporate hedging, academic speak.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Just say it directly)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "It's important to note that tours run rain or shine"
- ✅ "Tours run rain or shine"
- ❌ "It's worth mentioning we can accommodate dietary needs"
- ✅ "Vegetarian? Halal? Allergies? Just tell us."

---

#### ❌ Embark / Commence / Partake
**Why forbidden:** Victorian English, formal, nobody talks like this.

**What friends say instead:**
- Start, begin, join, come, eat

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Embark on this culinary journey"
- ✅ "Come eat with us"
- ❌ "Commence your tour"
- ✅ "Start your tour" OR "Meet us at..."

---

### Category 7: Generic Adjectives

#### ❌ Unique
**Why forbidden:** Overused, usually not true, lazy writing.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Explain what actually makes it different)

**Examples:**
- ❌ "A unique culinary experience"
- ✅ "Peranakan fusion you can't find anywhere else"
- ❌ "Unique food tour"

---

#### ❌ Special
**Why forbidden:** Vague, kindergarten teacher language.

**What friends say instead:**
- (Say why it matters)

**Examples:**

- ✅ "A food tour where you meet vendors our families have known for decades"
- ❌ "Special cultural insights"

## THE GOLDEN RULES

### Rule 1: Show, Don't Claim
❌ "Authentic Malaysian food"
✅ "Aunty Lim's laksa, same recipe since 1982"

### Rule 2: If Tourism Industry Says It, Don't
❌ "Embark on a luxury culinary adventure"
✅ "Come eat at the stalls we've been going to since childhood"

### Rule 3: Would You Text This to a Friend?
❌ "We provide immersive cultural experiences"
✅ "Want to eat where we actually eat?"

### Rule 4: Specific > Generic
❌ "Unique local flavors"
✅ "Tangy tamarind, fiery sambal, coconut milk so fresh it's still warm"

### Rule 5: Proof > Claims
❌ "Best food tour in Malaysia"
✅ "TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2023, 1,250+ guests, 4.9 rating"

## BEFORE YOU PUBLISH - CHECKLIST

Read your copy and ask:

- [ ] Did I use any words from the forbidden list?
- [ ] Am I claiming or showing?
- [ ] Would I actually say this to a friend?
- [ ] Could any tour company say this exact thing?
- [ ] Did I use specific details (names, ages, years)?
- [ ] Did I use sensory language (taste, smell, sound)?
- [ ] Is there a real person in this sentence?
- [ ] Would this survive the "text message test"?

If you fail any check, rewrite.

## EXAMPLES: BEFORE & AFTER

### Example 1: Homepage Hero
❌ **Before:**
"Embark on an unforgettable culinary adventure through authentic Malaysia. Discover world-class flavors on this immersive cultural journey."

✅ **After:**
"Hungry for the Malaysia tourists miss? Eat where we actually eat. Meet vendors who've become family. Leave knowing why it matters."

---

### Example 2: Tour Description
❌ **Before:**
"Experience premium Malaysian cuisine with exclusive access to genuine local establishments. Our professional guides provide authentic cultural immersion."

✅ **After:**
"Walk through neighborhoods where we grew up. Meet Aunty Lim, 68, who's made laksa since 1982. Taste dishes our grandmothers taught us to love."

---

### Example 3: About Page
❌ **Before:**
"Simply Enak offers world-class culinary adventures featuring authentic Malaysian experiences. Our professional team provides immersive cultural journeys."

✅ **After:**
"In 2011, we got tired of watching tourists eat hotel food while incredible stalls sat two blocks away. So we started walking people through the neighborhoods where we grew up eating."

---

### Example 4: CTA
❌ **Before:**
"Embark on your premium culinary adventure today. Discover authentic Malaysia."

✅ **After:**
"Ready to eat? Join us."

## KEEP THIS GUIDE OPEN WHEN WRITING

Every single piece of copy should pass the forbidden words check.

**Remember:** Your voice is your competitive advantage. AI can write tour descriptions. Only you can write like a friend showing their favorite laksa spot to someone visiting from out of town.
