# Copywriting Guidelines: Brand Voice & Style

## Writing Principles

### Sentence Structure to Avoid
**DO NOT USE "it's not just X, it's Y" patterns**
- Instead of: "It's not just a food tour, it's a cultural journey"
- Use: "Experience authentic cultural connections through food"

**DO NOT USE EM-DASHES (—)**
- Instead of: "Our guides—locals who love sharing their home—take you to hidden gems"
- Use: "Our guides are locals who love sharing their home. They take you to hidden gems"

### Identity Statements
**AVOID**: "We're not Tour Operators"
**INSTEAD USE**: "We are locals who love sharing our home"

### Brand Voice Principles
1. **Positive Framing**: Focus on what you ARE, not what you're NOT
2. **Direct Communication**: Use simple, declarative sentences
3. **Authentic Language**: Write like you speak to a friend
4. **Cultural Focus**: Emphasize sharing, connection, and authenticity

## Approved Phrases
- "We share our culture, our stories, our home"
- "Experience Malaysia through the eyes of those who call it home"
- "Connect with the living heart of Malaysian culture"
- "Where locals actually eat, not where guidebooks send you"
- "We preserve and share the living heritage of our multicultural home"

## Strategic Use of "Authentic"

### ✅ When to Use "Authentic"
**SEO-Critical Places:**
- Title tags: "Kuala Lumpur Food Tours | Authentic Street Food"
- Meta descriptions for search engines
- Google Business Profile descriptions
- High-intent keywords where customers search for this term

**Differentiating Context:**
- "These aren't tourist restaurants - they're authentic family establishments"
- "Traditional cooking methods preserved through generations"
- "Cultural authenticity verified by local communities"

### ❌ When to Avoid "Authentic"
**In General Descriptions:**
- Don't just say "We serve authentic food"
- Don't overuse in marketing copy
- Don't use without specific context or proof

### ✅ Show Authenticity Instead
**Use Specific Details:**
- "Aunty Lim has been making laksa here since 1982 using her grandmother's recipe"
- "These are our family's favorite spots, where we've eaten for generations"
- "Our guides grew up in these neighborhoods"
- "Where families have been eating for 40+ years"
- "Traditional cooking methods that haven't changed"

## Phrases to Avoid
- "It's not just a tour, it's..."
- "We're not tour operators, we're..."
- "Don't be a tourist, be a traveler"
- "This isn't your typical food tour"
- Any sentence using em-dashes for emphasis

## Writing Style
- Use short, clear sentences
- Break up complex ideas into multiple sentences
- Focus on authentic experiences and cultural connections
- Write in warm, enthusiastic tone
- Avoid comparative statements that diminish others
