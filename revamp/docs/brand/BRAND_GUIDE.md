# Simply Enak Brand Voice Guide

## The Core Principle

**Write like a passionate friend sharing their favorite food spot — not a tourism brochure.**

Ask yourself before every sentence:
1. Would I text this to a friend?
2. Does it have a real person in it (name, age, years)?
3. Am I showing or claiming?
4. Could any tour company say this?

## Voice Rules

**Use:**
- Short, direct sentences
- Sensory details (crispy, aromatic, spicy, tangy)
- Real names, ages, years, specific dishes
- "Come eat with us", "Join us", "Walk with us"
- "And", "But", "Plus" (not "Moreover", "Furthermore")
- Guests, friends, you (not "customers", "clients")
- Stall, shop, place, spot (not "establishment", "venue")

**Never use:**
- Em-dashes for dramatic pauses
- "We're not X, we're Y" contrast patterns
- "However", "Moreover", "Additionally", "Furthermore"
- "Embark on a journey", "Immersive experience"
- "Authentic", "Genuine", "Premium", "Luxury", "World-class"
- "Must-see", "Can't-miss", "Amazing", "Incredible", "Unforgettable"
- "It's important to note", "It's worth mentioning"
- "Customer", "Client", "Establishment", "Venue"
- Passive voice ("Tours are conducted")
- Corporate hedging ("One might consider")

## Quick Word Swaps

| Instead of | Say |
|-----------|-----|
| Adventure | Tour, walk, time with us |
| Journey | Tour, walk, visit |
| Discover | See, meet, taste, find |
| Explore | Walk through, visit, eat at |
| Experience (vague) | Tour, or be specific: eat, taste, meet |
| Immersive | Just describe what happens |
| Authentic | Show through details (names, years) |
| Premium | Small groups, experienced guides, 14+ years |
| Best | Use proof: awards, ratings, reviews |
| Customer | Guest, friend, you |
| Provide | Show, share, take you to |
| However | And, but, plus |

## Before & After Examples

**Tour description:**
- Bad: "Embark on an unforgettable culinary adventure through authentic Malaysia. Discover world-class flavors on this immersive cultural journey."
- Good: "Hungry for the Malaysia tourists miss? Eat where we actually eat. Meet vendors who've become family."

**About page:**
- Bad: "Simply Enak was founded with the vision of sharing authentic Malaysian culture through food."
- Good: "In 2011, we got frustrated watching tourists eat hotel food while incredible stalls sat two blocks away. So we started walking people through the neighborhoods where we grew up eating."

**CTA:**
- Bad: "We invite you to embark on this culinary journey with us."
- Good: "Ready to eat? Join us."

**Vendor spotlight:**
- Bad: "Our tours feature authentic local vendors who demonstrate traditional cooking techniques."
- Good: "Meet Aunty Lim, 68. She's been making laksa at this stall since 1982. Her daughter helps now, learning the recipe Aunty Lim's mother taught her."

## Approved Phrases

- "We share our culture, our stories, our home"
- "Experience Malaysia through the eyes of those who call it home"
- "Where locals actually eat, not where guidebooks send you"
- "Come eat with us"
- "Walk with us through our neighborhoods"

## Content Pillars

1. **Tailored Experiences** — small groups, dietary flexibility, personal attention
2. **Malaysia's Beauty** — cultural heritage, food stories, vendor relationships
3. **Sustainable Impact** — fair vendor pay, community relationships, 14+ years

## Success Filter

Every piece of content should make visitors feel: **WELCOMED, FASCINATED, CONNECTED**
