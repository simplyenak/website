## Profile-Boosting Questions (NEW — Add to Interview)

### Section 11: Press & Media (10 minutes)

**For Media Coverage & Backlinks:**

61. **What's your most memorable press interview?**
    - Who interviewed you?
    - What did they focus on?
    - Can we republish or reference it?

62. **Which journalists or publications should we target?**
    - Who has covered similar businesses?
    - What's their angle?
    - Do you have any contacts?

63. **What's a controversial take on Malaysian food tourism?**
    - Something that would generate discussion
    - A myth you'd like to debunk
    - An unpopular opinion

64. **What awards or recognition have you received?**
    - TripAdvisor certificates
    - Local tourism awards
    - Media mentions
    - Industry recognition

65. **What makes Simply Enak newsworthy right now?**
    - Any recent milestones?
    - Unique initiatives?
    - Community impact?

---

### Section 12: Partnerships & Collaborations (10 minutes)

**For Cross-Promotion & Reach:**

66. **Which hotels do you work with or want to work with?**
    - Current partnerships
    - Dream partnerships
    - What value do you offer them?

67. **Which travel bloggers or influencers have you hosted?**
    - Who had the best experience?
    - Did they create content?
    - Can we repurpose it?

68. **Which complementary businesses should we partner with?**
    - Other tour operators (non-competing)
    - Hotels, hostels, Airbnb hosts
    - Travel agencies
    - Airlines

69. **Have you ever collaborated with tourism boards?**
    - Tourism Malaysia?
    - Visit KL?
    - Tourism Penang?
    - What did you do together?

70. **What's a collaboration you'd love to do?**
    - Dream partnership?
    - Why haven't you done it yet?
    - How can we make it happen?

---

### Section 13: Community & Sustainability (10 minutes)

**For CSR & Brand Reputation:**

71. **How do you give back to the community?**
    - Do you support vendors financially?
    - Do you mentor other guides?
    - Do you participate in local initiatives?

72. **What sustainability practices do you follow?**
    - Waste reduction?
    - Supporting local businesses?
    - Carbon offsetting?
    - Fair wages?

73. **How do you ensure vendors are treated fairly?**
    - Do you pay above market rate?
    - Do you bring consistent business?
    - Do you help them in other ways?

74. **What's your impact on the neighborhoods you tour?**
    - Economic impact?
    - Cultural preservation?
    - Gentrification concerns?

75. **What's a vendor whose life you've helped change?**
    - Specific story
    - Before/after
    - Quantifiable impact

---

### Section 14: Innovation & Industry Leadership (10 minutes)

**For Thought Leadership:**

76. **What do other tour operators copy from you?**
    - What have you pioneered?
    - What's your unique methodology?
    - What's your secret sauce?

77. **What's wrong with the food tour industry?**
    - What do competitors do poorly?
    - What would you change?
    - What should guests watch out for?

78. **Where is food tourism heading?**
    - Trends you're seeing
    - Changes since 2011
    - Predictions for next 5 years

79. **What do you wish guests knew before booking ANY food tour?**
    - Industry-wide advice
    - Red flags to watch for
    - Questions to ask

80. **If you were teaching a masterclass on food tours, what would you teach?**
    - Your methodology
    - Your principles
    - Your best practices

---

### Section 15: Guest Success Stories (10 minutes)

**For Social Proof & Testimonials:**

81. **What's the best review you've ever received?**
    - Specific guest quote
    - What made it special?
    - Can we feature it prominently?

82. **Which guest had the most transformative experience?**
    - Who were they?
    - What changed for them?
    - Can we interview them?

83. **Have any guests become friends?**
    - How did that happen?
    - Do you still keep in touch?
    - What's the story?

84. **Have you hosted any notable people?**
    - Celebrities, CEOs, politicians?
    - Can we mention them (even anonymously)?
    - What was their reaction?

85. **What's a guest story that makes you cry?**
    - Emotional moment
    - Why did it affect you?
    - Can we share it?

---

### Section 16: Social Media & Viral Moments (5 minutes)

**For Shareable Content:**

86. **What's your most viral social media post?**
    - What was it?
    - Why did it resonate?
    - Can we create more like it?

87. **What's a photo that always gets likes?**
    - Which dish?
    - Which vendor?
    - Which moment?

88. **What's something guests always photograph?**
    - The "Instagrammable" moment
    - Why do they love it?
    - Can we lean into it?

89. **What's a behind-the-scenes moment people would love?**
    - Prep work
    - Vendor interactions
    - Tour guide secrets

90. **If you could create one viral moment, what would it be?**
    - Stunt, event, or campaign
    - Why would it work?
    - What's stopping you?
