## FAQ (200 words)

### What's a wet market?

A wet market is a traditional fresh food market common across Southeast Asia. "Wet" refers to the wet floors from melted ice, fish water, and regular washing. You'll find fresh produce, meats, seafood, and prepared foods. It's the opposite of a dry supermarket — sensory, chaotic, alive.

### Is it safe to eat at wet markets?

Yes, generally very safe. Wet markets have high turnover (food is fresh), vendors take pride in their reputation, and locals shop there daily. The biggest risk is sensitive stomachs adjusting to new foods. We take you to stalls we personally trust, with clean practices and high turnover.

### What should I wear on a market tour?

Comfortable clothes you don't mind getting a bit dirty, comfortable walking shoes (floors can be wet), and an empty stomach. It gets hot and humid — dress light. Bring a camera, cash for purchases, and an open mind.

### Can Simply Enak accommodate dietary requirements on market tours?

Yes, completely. We've been accommodating vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and vegan guests for 14 years. We know which stalls offer suitable options, which ingredients to avoid, which vendors can explain their preparation methods. Just let us know when you book.
