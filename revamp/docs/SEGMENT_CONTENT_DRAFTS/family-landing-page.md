## FAQ (200 words)

### What's the best age for a family food tour?

Ages 6+ work best — kids can walk the distance, are willing to try new foods, and can engage with the cultural experience. Younger kids (3-5) can join but may find the 3-hour duration challenging. Teenagers usually love it — especially if they're foodies. We've had multi-generational families from age 5 to 75 — all enjoyed it.

### My child is a picky eater. Will there be anything they'll eat?

Yes, definitely. Malaysian food has many kid-friendly options: rice dishes, noodles, grilled meats, fresh fruits. We know which dishes are mild, which are familiar formats, which vendors can make modifications. We've never had a kid go hungry — and we've had many picky eaters try something new.

### Are the tours stroller-friendly?

Partially. Some areas are stroller-friendly (paved paths, wide sidewalks), but some old neighborhoods have narrow lanes and steps. We recommend baby carriers for very young children. If you bring a stroller, we'll plan a route that works — but it may limit some areas we can visit.

### Can Simply Enak accommodate dietary requirements for kids?

Yes, completely. We've been accommodating families with vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and allergic kids for 14 years. We know which dishes are safe, which vendors understand allergies, which can make modifications. Just let us know when you book.
