## FAQ (200 words)

### What's the difference between a regular food tour and a foodie tour?

Regular food tours focus on tasting and enjoyment. Foodie tours go deeper — techniques, history, cultural context, regional variations. More dishes, more stalls, more time for questions. Regular tours are great for first-timers. Foodie tours are for guests who want to understand Malaysian food at a deeper level.

### Can I photograph the cooking process?

Yes! We encourage photography. Most vendors are happy to be photographed (we always ask first). Some of the best foodie photos happen when vendors are in their element — flipping roti, stir-frying char kway teow, assembling laksa. We'll position you for the best shots.

### Do you offer cooking demonstrations?

Some vendors do informal demonstrations — showing you how to flip roti canai, explaining their spice paste preparation, letting you try your hand at techniques. This isn't a formal cooking class, but many vendors love to share their craft with appreciative guests.

### Can Simply Enak accommodate dietary requirements on foodie tours?

Yes, completely. We've been accommodating vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, and Jain guests for 14 years. Foodie tours actually work well for dietary requirements — we can explain how dishes are modified, what ingredients to look for, which techniques work for different diets. Just let us know when you book.
