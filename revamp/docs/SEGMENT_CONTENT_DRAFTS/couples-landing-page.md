## FAQ (200 words)

### Are food tours good for couples on honeymoon?

Yes, absolutely! Food tours are perfect for honeymooners — you're experiencing something new together, creating shared memories, and eating amazing food. We've had many honeymooners on our tours. We can arrange special touches (champagne at sunset, special vendor introductions) for honeymooners. Just let us know when you book.

### What's the best time for a couples food tour?

Sunset tours (5pm start) are most romantic — golden hour for photos, cooler temperatures, and you'll end the tour in the evening when the atmosphere is most romantic. Evening tours (7pm start) are also great — night markets, romantic lighting, and you can continue the evening at a rooftop bar after.

### Can we do a private couples-only tour?

Yes! Private tours are perfect for couples. You'll have the guide to yourselves, complete flexibility on pace and stops, and we can build in special romantic moments. Many couples choose private tours for honeymoons, anniversaries, or proposals (yes, we've been part of proposals!).

### Is it appropriate to hold hands, be affectionate on the tour?

Yes, within reason. Malaysia is moderately conservative — holding hands is fine, especially in tourist areas. More intimate affection should be kept private. Your guide will advise on cultural norms. Most vendors are used to tourist couples and are very welcoming.
