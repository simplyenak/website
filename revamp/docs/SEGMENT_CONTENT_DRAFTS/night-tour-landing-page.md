## FAQ (200 words)

### Is it safe to do a night food tour in KL?

Yes, very safe when done with a local guide. We take you to well-established, well-lit areas with high foot traffic. KL's city center and main neighborhoods are safe at night. We've been running night tours for 14 years with no safety issues. Just use common sense and stay with the group.

### What's the difference between day and night food tours?

Day tours focus on breakfast/lunch dishes (nasi lemak, dim sum, morning markets). Night tours focus on dinner/late-night dishes (satay, char kway teow, night markets). Different vendors, different atmosphere, different energy. Both are excellent — day tours are more cultural, night tours are more social.

### What should I wear on a night food tour?

Comfortable clothes, comfortable walking shoes, and layers (it can be cooler at night). Bring a camera, cash for purchases, and an empty stomach. Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered) for cultural respect.

### Can Simply Enak accommodate dietary requirements on night tours?

Yes, completely. We've been accommodating vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and vegan guests for 14 years. Night vendors are often more flexible with modifications. Just let us know when you book.
