## Understanding Jain Requirements in Malaysia (250 words)

### What Makes Food Jain-Safe?

**No Meat (Obviously)**
- No beef, pork, chicken, fish, shellfish
- This is easy in Malaysia — many vegetarian options

**No Root Vegetables (The Challenge)**
- No onion, garlic, shallots, leeks, chives
- No potatoes, carrots, radishes, turnips
- No ginger, turmeric root (above-ground parts OK)
- Why: Root vegetables kill the entire plant when harvested

**No Eggs (Most Jain Diets)**
- No eggs, no egg products
- Some Jain diets allow dairy (lacto-vegetarian)
- Some exclude dairy too (vegan Jain)

**No Certain Fruits (Strict Jain)**
- No figs (contains wasps)
- Some avoid multi-seeded fruits
- Varies by Jain tradition

### Where to Find Jain Food in Malaysia

**Chinese Buddhist Vegetarian Stalls (Naturally Jain-Friendly)**
- Already exclude onion and garlic
- Use mushrooms, tofu, vegetables for flavor
- Found in Chinatown, Buddhist temple canteens
- Most are naturally Jain-safe (verify root vegetables)

**Indian Jain Restaurants**
- Owned by Jain community members
- Understand Jain requirements deeply
- Found in KL's Little India, Penang's Georgetown
- 100% Jain-safe by default

**Indian Vegetarian Restaurants (Can Modify)**
- Understand vegetarian requirements
- Can often make Jain modifications
- Request no onion, no garlic, no root vegetables
- Common in Brickfields (KL), Jalan Masjid Kapitan Keling (Penang)
