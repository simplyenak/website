## FAQ (200 words)

### What's the difference between heritage food and regular Malaysian food?

Heritage food refers to recipes and techniques passed down through generations, often tied to specific cultural traditions. In Penang, this means Peranakan (Nyonya) cuisine, third-generation hawker stalls, and recipes that haven't changed in 50+ years. Regular Malaysian food includes modern interpretations and newer establishments.

### Why is Penang food different from KL food?

Penang has stronger Peranakan influence, more seafood (island location), and older hawker traditions (some stalls 60+ years old). KL has more Malay and Indian influence, more modern fusion, and newer establishments. Both are excellent — Penang is more heritage-focused, KL is more diverse.

### Can Simply Enak accommodate dietary requirements on heritage tours?

Yes, completely. We've been accommodating vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and vegan guests for 14 years. Many heritage dishes can be modified, and we know which vendors understand dietary requirements. Just let us know when you book.

### What's the best time for a heritage food tour?

Morning (9am-12pm) is best — vendors are fresh, food is made daily, and it's cooler for walking. Afternoon (3pm-6pm) is also good — some vendors only open for dinner. We'll time the tour for the best experience.
