## Content Notes by Segment

### Vegetarian
**Key Points:**
- Buddhist vegetarian (no egg, no dairy, no onion/garlic)
- Indian vegetarian (egg/dairy OK, no meat)
- Hidden ingredients (belacan, fish sauce, oyster sauce)
- 素食 (sù shí) stalls

**Vendors to Mention:**
- Buddhist vegetarian stalls in Chinatown
- Indian vegetarian restaurants in Brickfields

### Halal
**Key Points:**
- Malaysia is Muslim-majority country
- Most Malay food is halal
- JAKIM certification
- Chinese food often not halal

**Vendors to Mention:**
- JAKIM-certified establishments
- Malay food stalls

### Gluten-Free
**Key Points:**
- Rice-based dishes naturally GF
- Soy sauce contains wheat
- Rice noodles (bee hoon) usually GF
- Cross-contamination risks

**Safe Dishes:**
- Nasi lemak (rice)
- Satay (check marinade)
- Fresh fruits

### Vegan
**Key Points:**
- Different from Buddhist vegetarian
- Modern vegan cafes growing
- Traditional vegan options exist
- Egg noodles vs. rice noodles

### Jain
**Key Points:**
- No root vegetables (onion, garlic, potatoes, carrots)
- Very strict vegetarian
- Rare but can accommodate
- Need advance notice

### Street Food
**Key Points:**
- Hawker centers vs. street stalls
- Health regulations
- Best areas (Chinatown, Jalan Alor)
- What to expect

### Market Tour
**Key Points:**
- Wet markets vs. dry markets
- Morning markets best
- Chow Kit Market (KL)
- Chowrasta Market (Penang)

### Heritage
**Key Points:**
- UNESCO Georgetown
- Peranakan culture
- Generational vendors
- Recipe preservation

### Night Tour
**Key Points:**
- Night markets (pasar malam)
- Late-night hawker stalls
- Safety considerations
- Best areas after dark

### Family
**Key Points:**
- Kid-friendly dishes
- Age recommendations
- Pace adjustments
- Bathroom breaks

### Couples
**Key Points:**
- Romantic settings
- Private tour options
- Sunset timing
- Photo opportunities

### Solo
**Key Points:**
- Join-in tours available
- Meet other travelers
- Safety tips
- Single supplement (none for food tours)

### Foodie
**Key Points:**
- Deep cultural context
- Vendor techniques
- Rare dishes
- Behind-the-scenes access
