No! Unlike many tours that charge extra for solo travelers, we charge the same per-person rate regardless of group size. You're paying for the experience, not the number of people in your party. Solo travelers are welcome on all our join-in tours at no extra cost.

### Will I be the only solo traveler on the tour?

Rarely. Most tours have 2-4 solo travelers, plus couples and small groups. Solo travelers often become the heart of the group — they're more open to conversation, more willing to try new things, and more likely to make lasting friendships. You'll fit right in.
