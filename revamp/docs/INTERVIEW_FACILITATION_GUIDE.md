## 🎯 Interview Structure (90 Minutes)

### Opening (5 minutes)

**Script:**
> "Thanks for doing this! The goal today is to capture all the stories, vendor relationships, and cultural insights that make Simply Enak unique. This 90-minute conversation will give us enough material for 50+ pages of content.
>
> There are no wrong answers. If I ask a question and you're not sure, just say so. If a question sparks a story, tell it — those stories are gold.
>
> I'll be taking notes and may interrupt with follow-up questions like 'What's the vendor's name?' or 'How long have they been there?' — those specifics are what we need.
>
> Ready to start?"

---

### Section 1: Brand Story & Philosophy (10 minutes)

**Goal:** Capture origin story, differentiation, unique value

**Questions:**

**Q1: Why did you start Simply Enak in 2011?**
- What was the specific moment or experience?
- What were you doing before Simply Enak?

**Follow-ups:**
- "What was the rejection story with the tour company?"
- "What did IT/corporate life feel like vs. what you wanted?"
- "What was the first tour like?"

**Q2: What's the biggest misconception tourists have about Malaysian food?**
- What do they expect vs. what they actually experience?

**Follow-ups:**
- "Can you give a specific example?"
- "What dish do they always misunderstand?"

**Q3: What makes Simply Enak different from other food tours?**
- Not "we're authentic" — what do you actually DO differently?
- What can guests experience with you that they can't get on their own?

**Follow-ups:**
- "What's a specific vendor relationship that shows this?"
- "What's something you do that Chef's Tour doesn't?"

**Q4: Tell me about a guest experience that surprised you.**
- A moment when you saw a guest's perspective change
- A reaction you didn't expect

**Follow-ups:**
- "What tour was this on?"
- "Which vendor was involved?"
- "What did the guest say afterwards?"

---

### Section 2: Vendor Relationships (20 minutes)

**Goal:** Capture specific vendor names, stories, techniques

**For each vendor, capture:**
- Name (e.g., "Aunty Lim", not "a local vendor")
- Stall location
- What they make
- How long they've been doing it
- How you met them
- What makes their version special
- A specific detail about their technique or story

**Questions:**

**Q5: Who are your top 5 vendor relationships?**
- For each: Name, stall location, what they make, how long you've known them

**Q6: Which vendor has the best story?**
- Family history, recipe origin, challenges they've overcome

**Q7: Which dish requires the most skill to make?**
- What's the technique that takes years to master?
- Who does it best?

**Q8: Are there any vendors who are retiring soon?**
- Is their recipe being passed down?
- Is there a succession story?

**Q9: Which vendor do guests connect with most?**
- Why do they resonate with this person?
- What's the interaction like?

**Q10: Have any vendors become friends (not just business relationships)?**
- How did that happen?
- What's the friendship like?

**Critical Follow-ups (ask for EVERY vendor mentioned):**
- "What's their full name or what do guests call them?"
- "How long have they been at that stall?"
- "What's their signature dish called?"
- "What's one thing they do differently from others?"
- "Do they have a photo we can use?"

---

### Section 3: Dietary Accommodations (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Capture specific challenges, solutions, trusted vendors

**Questions:**

**Q11: What's the biggest challenge for vegetarians in Malaysia?**
- Specific ingredients to watch for (shrimp paste, fish sauce, etc.)
- Dishes that seem vegetarian but aren't

**Q12: Which vendors do you trust for vegetarian food?**
- Name specific stalls/restaurants
- How do you know they're truly vegetarian?

**Q13: What's a vegetarian dish that even meat-eaters love?**
- Specific dish, specific vendor

**Q14: For halal observers: what should they know about Malaysian food?**
- Is most food already halal?
- What are the exceptions?
- How do you verify halal status?

**Q15: For gluten-free: what are the hidden sources of gluten?**
- Soy sauce, oyster sauce, etc.
- Which dishes are naturally gluten-free?

**Q16: For Jain: what's different from regular vegetarian?**
- No root vegetables?
- How do you accommodate this?

**Q17: Tell me about a time you accommodated a difficult dietary need.**
- What was the challenge?
- How did you solve it?
- What was the guest's reaction?

**Critical Follow-ups:**
- "What's the Malay/Chinese name for that ingredient?"
- "Which specific stall handles this well?"
- "What do you tell guests to watch for?"

### Section 4: Location-Specific Insights (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Capture KL vs. Penang differences, neighborhood specifics

**Questions:**

- Which dishes are better in which city?
- Why did each city develop differently?

**Q19: In KL: which neighborhood has the best street food?**
- Why that neighborhood?
- What's the history?

**Q20: In Penang: what makes Georgetown special?**
- UNESCO heritage — how does that affect the food?
- Which dishes are uniquely Penang?

**Q21: What's a dish you can only get in KL?**
- Specific dish, specific vendor

**Q22: What's a dish you can only get in Penang?**
- Specific dish, specific vendor

**Q23: If a guest only has one day in KL, what should they eat?**
- 3-5 specific dishes, specific places

**Q24: If a guest only has one day in Penang, what should they eat?**
- 3-5 specific dishes, specific places

**Critical Follow-ups:**
- "What's the dish called in Malay/Chinese?"
- "Where exactly is that stall?"
- "What's the vendor's name?"
- "Why is it different in KL vs. Penang?"

## 🎯 Interview Tips

### Do's

✅ **Ask for specifics:**
- "What's their name?"
- "How long have they been there?"
- "What's the dish called?"

✅ **Follow story threads:**
- If a question sparks a story, let them tell it
- Those stories are gold for content

✅ **Interrupt for clarity:**
- "Wait, what's the spelling of that name?"
- "Which neighborhood is that stall in?"

✅ **Capture emotions:**
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "What did the guest say?"

### Don'ts

❌ **Don't rush:**
- 90 minutes is enough
- Let pauses happen

❌ **Don't accept generic answers:**
- If they say "authentic," ask "What specifically makes it authentic?"
- If they say "local vendors," ask "Which vendors? What are their names?"

❌ **Don't skip follow-ups:**
- The follow-ups are where the gold is
- Keep asking "What else?" "Tell me more"
