**Purpose:** Gather the specific stories, vendor names, and cultural insights needed to create unique, helpful content for 1,035 programmatic pages.

## Section 1: Brand Story & Philosophy (10 minutes)

### Simply Enak's Origin
1. **Why did you start Simply Enak in 2011?**
   - What was the specific moment or experience that made you think "I should do this"?
   - What were you doing before Simply Enak?

2. **What's the biggest misconception tourists have about Malaysian food?**
   - What do they expect vs. what they actually experience?

3. **What makes Simply Enak different from other food tours?**
   - Not "we're authentic" — what do you actually DO differently?
   - What can guests experience with you that they can't get on their own?

4. **Tell me about a guest experience that surprised you.**
   - A moment when you saw a guest's perspective change
   - A reaction you didn't expect

## Section 2: Vendor Relationships (20 minutes)

### Specific Vendors & Their Stories

**For each vendor, I need:**

- How long they've been doing it
- How you met them

- A specific detail about their technique or story

5. **Who are your top 5 vendor relationships?**

8. **Are there any vendors who are retiring soon?**

9. **Which vendor do guests connect with most?**

10. **Have any vendors become friends (not just business relationships)?**

## Section 3: Dietary Accommodations (15 minutes)

### Vegetarian/Vegan/Halal/Gluten-Free/Jain

11. **What's the biggest challenge for vegetarians in Malaysia?**
    - Specific ingredients to watch for (shrimp paste, fish sauce, etc.)
    - Dishes that seem vegetarian but aren't

12. **Which vendors do you trust for vegetarian food?**
    - Name specific stalls/restaurants
    - How do you know they're truly vegetarian (vs. just having vegetarian options)?

13. **What's a vegetarian dish that even meat-eaters love?**
    - Specific dish, specific vendor

14. **For halal observers: what should they know about Malaysian food?**
    - Is most food already halal?
    - What are the exceptions?
    - How do you verify halal status?

15. **For gluten-free: what are the hidden sources of gluten?**
    - Soy sauce, oyster sauce, etc.
    - Which dishes are naturally gluten-free?

16. **For Jain: what's different from regular vegetarian?**
    - No root vegetables?
    - How do you accommodate this?

17. **Tell me about a time you accommodated a difficult dietary need.**
    - What was the challenge?
    - How did you solve it?
    - What was the guest's reaction?

## How to Use This Interview

### Before the Interview:
1. Schedule 60-90 minutes with Pauline and/or Maarten
2. Set up recording (Zoom, phone recorder, etc.)
3. Share questions in advance (optional — spontaneous answers are often better)

### During the Interview:
1. Record the entire conversation
2. Ask follow-up questions when stories emerge
3. Push for specifics: "What's the vendor's name?" "How long have they been there?" "What's the specific technique?"
4. Don't accept generic answers: If they say "authentic," ask "What specifically makes it authentic?"

### After the Interview:
1. Transcribe the recording (use Otter.ai, Rev.com, or similar)
2. Extract specific vendor names, dish names, and stories
3. Organize by page type (dietary, specialty, location, etc.)
4. Use as source material for content creation
