## Content Template (Per Page)

### 1. Hero Section (100-150 words)
- **Hook:** What makes this segment special in Malaysia
- **Challenge:** Specific challenges for this segment (e.g., shrimp paste for vegetarians)
- **Solution:** How Simply Enak accommodates this need
- **Specific vendors:** Name 1-2 specific vendors and their dishes

### 2. What to Expect (300-400 words)
- **3-5 specific dishes** they'll eat
- **3-5 specific experiences** they'll have
- **Cultural context** — why this matters in Malaysia
- **Vendor stories** — name specific vendors, how long they've been doing it

### 3. Challenges & Solutions (200-300 words)
- **Challenge 1:** [Specific challenge]
  - **Solution:** [How Simply Enak handles it]
- **Challenge 2:** [Specific challenge]
  - **Solution:** [How Simply Enak handles it]

### 4. FAQ (5-7 questions)
Use questions from interview transcript. Examples:
- "Is [segment] food available in Malaysia?"
- "What dishes can I eat?"
- "How do you ensure [dietary need] is met?"
- "What if I have multiple [requirements]?"

### 5. Recommended Tours (100-150 words)
- **2-3 specific tours** that are great for this segment
- **Why each tour works** for this segment
- **Link to tour detail pages**

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### DO:
- ✅ Use specific names (Aunty Lim, Master Chen, not "local vendors")
- ✅ Use specific dish names (char kway teow, not "local noodles")
- ✅ Write like a friend sharing recommendations
- ✅ Include cultural context and stories
- ✅ Use contractions (you'll, we're, don't)
- ✅ One `!` max per paragraph

### DON'T:
- ❌ Use forbidden words: authentic, premium, luxury, discover, explore, immerse, customer, delicious, traditional (without story), unique, best, #1, must-see
- ❌ Use em-dashes (—)
- ❌ Use "it's not just X, it's Y" pattern
- ❌ Write like a tourism brochure
- ❌ Make generic claims without specifics

**Full brand voice guide:** See `docs/BRAND_VOICE_GUIDE.md`
