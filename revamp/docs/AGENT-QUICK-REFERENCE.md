# Agent Quick Reference: Experience Profiler Data

> Print this and keep it at your desk when handling chats

---

## What You'll See

When a guest opens a chat after completing the Experience Profiler, you'll see these **custom attributes** in the chat sidebar:

| Attribute | What It Means | How to Respond |
|---|---|---|
| `tour_motivation` | What drives them | Use this for your opening greeting |
| `tour_style` | How they like to experience | Adjust your pace and tone |
| `tour_memory_goal` | The story they want to take home | Highlight that aspect in your recommendation |
| `tour_openness` | How adventurous they are | Know how far outside comfort zone to push |
| `tour_city` | Where they want to go | Suggest specific routes |
| `tour_group_type` | Who they're traveling with | Adjust your recommendation |
| `tour_headcount` | How many people | Quote pricing accordingly |
| `tour_dietary` | Any dietary restrictions | Reassure them we handle this |
| `tour_referral` | How they found us | Track marketing effectiveness |
| `tour_dates` | When they're coming | Check availability |
| `tour_question` | Their specific question | Answer this first |

## Opening Responses by Motivation

### Authenticity Seeker (`authenticity`)
> "I see you're looking for the real Malaysia — the stalls locals eat at, not the tourist spots. 

We've been taking guests to the same family-run stalls for 14+ years. Tell me: which city are you exploring, and how many people in your group?

I'll suggest something that hits exactly the right hidden spots."

### Culture Explorer (`culture`)
> "Food is the best way to understand a place — we couldn't agree more. 

Every dish on our tours comes with the story behind it: who makes it, why it matters, and how it got here. 

Which city are you visiting, and is this your first time in Malaysia?"

### Milestone Maker (`milestone`)
> "Special trips deserve something special — I'd love to help you plan it. 

Our private tours are built entirely around you: your pace, your interests, your schedule. No rush, no crowds.

What's the occasion, and how many people will be joining?"

### Return Explorer (`return`)
> "Welcome back — or welcome to the deeper side of Malaysia! 

Since you've been before, I'll skip the basics and take you to the spots most visitors never find. The hidden stalls, the neighbourhoods off the tourist map.

Which city are you heading to, and how many in your group?"

### Chef / Culinary Enthusiast (`chef`)
> "I see you're here to learn the craft — not just eat. 

We can arrange visits to the people behind the food: the noodle maker, the belacan producer, the wok master who's been doing char kway teow for 30 years.

Which city, and what's your cooking background? I'll suggest the right route."

## City-Specific Lines

### Kuala Lumpur
- "We've got three routes through KL — from the street food of Pudu to the hidden lanes of Chow Kit Market."
- "KL's best food isn't on Jalan Alor — it's in the alleys behind it."
- "I'd start you with the KL Street Food route, but customize it based on what matters to you."

### Penang
- "George Town is our favourite — every stall in those pre-war shophouses has a story going back generations."
- "Penang's food scene is denser than anywhere else in Malaysia. We can hit 8-10 stops in 4 hours and still feel unhurried."
- "I'd start you with Eat Drink George Town — it's our signature route for a reason."

### Both Cities
- "KL and Penang in one trip — that's the full Malaysian food experience. We'll design a route that connects both cities."
- "If you're doing both, I'd suggest KL first (the introduction), then Penang (the deep dive)."

> "Team building over food — now that's a good idea. We've done this for companies before. Let me put together a proposal."

> "What's the occasion? I'll make sure the route has a special moment — maybe a hidden café stop, or a vendor who'll share their personal story."

## Tips from Experience

- **Use their name** if they provided email — look up past conversations
- **Reference their motivation** early — shows you actually read their profile
- **Suggest one route, then customize** — don't overwhelm with options
- **Mention dietary reassurance** — always confirm we brief vendors in advance
- **Close with a clear next step** — "Shall I sketch out a route for you?" or "Want to book this for a specific date?"
