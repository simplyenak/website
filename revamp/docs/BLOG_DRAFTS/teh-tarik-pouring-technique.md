## What Is Teh Tarik? (150 words)

### The Short Answer

**Teh tarik** literally means "pulled tea" in Malay.

**What's in it:**
- Strong black tea (usually Boh or local brand)
- Condensed milk (not evaporated — important!)
- Sugar (optional, but most add it)

**How it's made:**
1. Brew strong tea
2. Add condensed milk and sugar
3. Pour between two cups from high above
4. Repeat 3-4 times
5. Serve hot (or ask for "ais" for iced)

**Why pull it:**
- Cools the tea to drinkable temperature
- Creates frothy top layer
- Mixes tea and milk thoroughly
- Shows off the waiter's skill

### Where It Came From

**Origin:** Malaysia/Singapore, 1950s  
**Who created it:** Indian Muslim (Mamak) immigrants  
**Why:** To cool tea quickly in tropical heat

**Fun fact:** The pulling technique comes from Indian chai culture, but teh tarik is uniquely Malaysian.

## The Art of Pulling (200 words)

### Why Height Matters

**The higher the pour, the better the tea.**

**Here's why:**

**1. Aeration**
- Tea mixes with air as it falls
- Creates tiny bubbles
- Results in frothy, creamy top layer
- Better mouthfeel

**2. Cooling**
- Tea is brewed at 95°C+ (boiling)
- Condensed milk would curdle if added directly
- Pouring cools tea to ~65°C (drinkable)
- Perfect temperature for condensed milk

**3. Mixing**
- Tea and condensed milk have different densities
- Pouring ensures thorough mixing
- No stirring needed
- Consistent flavor throughout

## How to Order Teh Tarik (150 words)

### The Basics

**"Teh tarik, satu."** (One teh tarik)

**That's it.** You'll get hot teh tarik with standard sweetness.

### Customizations

**Temperature:**
- **"Teh tarik"** — Hot (default)
- **"Teh tarik ais"** — Iced
- **"Teh tarik suam"** — Warm (not too hot)

**Sweetness:**
- **"Kurang manis"** — Less sweet
- **"Manis biasa"** — Normal sweetness
- **"Tak manis"** — Not sweet (just condensed milk)

**Tea strength:**
- **"Teh tarik O"** — No milk (just tea and sugar)
- **"Teh tarik C"** — Evaporated milk instead of condensed (less sweet)

### What Not to Say

❌ **"Can you not pull it?"** — Yes they can, but why would you want flat tea?  
❌ **"Can you make it with almond milk?"** — This is mamak, not Starbucks  
❌ **"Can I have decaf?"** — It's tea, not coffee

## FAQ (100 words)

### Is teh tarik halal?

Yes, teh tarik at mamak stalls is halal. Condensed milk is dairy, tea is plant-based. No alcohol, no pork products.

### How much caffeine is in teh tarik?

About 40-50mg per cup (similar to a shot of espresso). Less than coffee, more than green tea.

### Can I get teh tarik without sugar?

Yes, order "teh tarik kosong" (empty, no sugar) or "kurang manis" (less sweet). But the condensed milk already has sugar...

### Why is my teh tarik so sweet?

Malaysian teh tarik is sweet by default. If you want less sweet, order "kurang manis." If you want it unsweetened, order "teh O kosong" (tea without milk or sugar).
