## 🎙️ Recording Setup Options

### Option A: Zoom (Recommended)

**Pros:**
- Auto-transcription available
- Easy to record
- Video + audio
- Can share screen if needed

**Setup:**
1. Create Zoom meeting
2. Enable cloud recording (Settings → Recording → Cloud recording)
3. Enable auto-transcription (Settings → Recording → Audio transcript)
4. Send invite to Pauline/Maarten
5. Start meeting 5 minutes early to test

**Recording Steps:**
1. Click "Record" button when interview starts
2. Click "Stop Recording" when done
3. Wait for Zoom to process (5-10 minutes)
4. Download transcript (.txt or .vtt file)
5. Download audio (.mp4 or .m4a)

---

### Option B: Otter.ai (Best Transcription)

**Pros:**
- Live transcription during interview
- 85-90% accurate
- Identifies different speakers
- Easy to search and export

**Setup:**
1. Create Otter.ai account (free tier: 300 minutes/month)
2. Install Otter mobile app or use web app
3. Start new recording in Otter
4. Place phone/laptop near speakers

**Recording Steps:**
1. Open Otter.ai app
2. Tap "+" → "Record Otter"
3. Start recording when interview starts
4. Stop when done
5. Export transcript (PDF, TXT, or DOCX)

---

### Option C: Phone Voice Recorder (Backup)

**Pros:**
- Always available
- No setup needed
- Reliable

**Cons:**
- No auto-transcription
- Need to upload to Rev.com or transcribe manually

**Setup:**
1. Open Voice Memos (iPhone) or Voice Recorder (Android)
2. Create new recording
3. Place phone near speakers
4. Test levels (speak for 10 seconds, playback)

## 🧠 Interviewer Mindset

### Your Role

**You are:**
- A curious friend asking great questions
- A facilitator drawing out stories
- A clarifier getting specifics (names, dates, places)

**You are NOT:**
- A journalist (don't need perfect quotes)
- A fact-checker (don't interrupt to verify)
- An editor (don't correct grammar or flow)

### When to Interrupt

**DO interrupt for:**
- "What's the spelling of that name?"
- "Which neighborhood is that?"
- "How long have they been there?"
- "What's the dish called in Malay/Chinese?"

**DON'T interrupt for:**
- Grammar corrections
- Going off on tangents (those are gold!)
- Pauses (let them think)

### When to Probe Deeper

**Ask "Tell me more" when:**
- They mention a vendor by name
- They reference a specific guest experience
- They describe a challenge they overcame
- They share an emotion ("That was the proudest moment...")

**Ask "What else?" when:**
- They finish a story
- They say "I can't think of anything else"
- You sense there's more to the story

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Technical Issues

**Problem:** Recording not working
**Solution:** Switch to phone voice recorder immediately, resume interview

**Problem:** Internet drops (Zoom)
**Solution:** Call in via phone, continue audio-only

**Problem:** Otter.ai not transcribing
**Solution:** Take manual notes, upload audio to Rev.com later

### Energy Issues

**Problem:** Pauline/Maarten running out of energy
**Solution:** Take break early, skip to Section 9 (easier questions)

**Problem:** Interviewer losing focus
**Solution:** Take 5-minute break, hydrate, refocus

### Content Issues

**Problem:** They can't remember a vendor's name
**Solution:** "No worries — we'll come back to it. What else do you remember?"

**Problem:** They give generic answers ("authentic", "local")
**Solution:** "What specifically makes it authentic? Can you give an example?"

**Problem:** They're rushing through stories
**Solution:** "Wait, I want to hear more about that. What happened next?"
