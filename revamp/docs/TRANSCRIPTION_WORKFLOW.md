# Interview Transcription Workflow

**Purpose:** Convert 90-minute interview into organized, searchable transcript for content creation

**Time Required:** 2-3 hours total  
**Tools Needed:** Recording software, transcription service, Google Docs

---

## Step 1: Record Interview (90 minutes)

### Setup:
- **Tool:** Zoom, phone recorder, or dedicated recorder
- **Format:** Audio + video (video helps with context)
- **Backup:** Record on two devices if possible
- **Location:** Quiet environment, minimal background noise

### During Interview:
- **Start:** State date, time, participants
- **Sections:** Announce each section ("Now moving to Section 2: Vendor Relationships")
- **Breaks:** Mark breaks clearly ("Taking a 2-minute break")
- **End:** State "End of interview" with date/time

---

## Step 2: Transcribe (60-90 minutes)

### Option A: Automated Service (Recommended)

**Tools:**
- **Otter.ai** — $10-30/month, 90% accuracy, speaker identification
- **Rev.com** — $1.50/minute, 99% accuracy, human transcription
- **Descript** — $12/month, 90% accuracy, editing features

**Process:**
1. Upload audio file
2. Wait for transcription (5-30 minutes)
3. Download transcript (TXT or DOCX)
4. Review for accuracy (15-30 minutes)

**Cost:** $10-135 depending on service

### Option B: Manual Transcription (Free but Time-Consuming)

**Tools:**
- **Google Docs** — Voice typing (free)
- **Express Scribe** — Free transcription software
- **Foot pedal** — $50-100 (optional, speeds up manual transcription)

**Process:**
1. Listen to 30 seconds
2. Type what was said
3. Repeat (60-90 minutes for 90-minute interview)

**Cost:** Free, but 2-3 hours of work

## Step 4: Extract Content Nuggets (30-60 minutes)

### Create Extraction Spreadsheet:

| Question | Content Type | Extract | Page Type | Priority |
|----------|-------------|---------|-----------|----------|
| Q5 | Vendor | "Aunty Lim, 68, laksa since 1982" | Dietary, KL | High |
| Q6 | Story | "Pak Din's satay recipe from grandmother" | Blog, Specialty | High |
| Q11 | Challenge | "Shrimp paste in vegetarian dishes" | Vegetarian page | High |
| Q12 | Vendor | "Vegetarian restaurant in Brickfields" | Vegetarian page | Medium |

### Extraction Categories:
1. **Vendor Names** — Names, ages, how long they've been cooking
2. **Dish Names** — Specific dishes, ingredients, preparation methods
3. **Stories** — Vendor stories, guest stories, origin stories
4. **Challenges** — Dietary challenges, guest concerns, solutions
5. **Statistics** — Years in business, guest counts, percentages
6. **Quotes** — Memorable quotes from Pauline/Maarten
7. **Cultural Insights** — Cultural context, traditions, history

---

## Step 5: Organize by Page Type (30 minutes)

### Create Folders:
```
/content-extraction/
├── dietary-pages/
│   ├── vegetarian.md
│   ├── halal.md
│   ├── gluten-free.md
│   ├── vegan.md
│   └── jain.md
├── specialty-pages/
│   ├── street-food.md
│   ├── market-tour.md
│   ├── heritage.md
│   └── night-tour.md
├── travel-type-pages/
│   ├── family.md
│   ├── couples.md
│   ├── solo.md
│   └── foodie.md
└── blog-posts/
    ├── post-1.md
    ├── post-2.md
    └── ...
```

### For Each Page:
1. Copy relevant extracts from spreadsheet
2. Organize by section (hero, what to expect, challenges, FAQ)
3. Add notes for writer ("Expand this story", "Get more details on X")
4. Save as Markdown file
