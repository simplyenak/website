## Content Quality Standards

### Every Page Must Have:

1. **Specific vendor names** — Not "local vendors" but "Aunty Lim's laksa stall"
2. **Specific dish names** — Not "local food" but "char kway teow with wok hei"
3. **Cultural context** — Why this matters, history, traditions
4. **Practical information** — What to expect, challenges, solutions
5. **Unique value** — Why Simply Enak is different
6. **Internal links** — 3-5 links to related content
7. **FAQ section** — 5-7 questions specific to this page
8. **Tour recommendations** — Curated, not all tours

### Every Page Must NOT Have:

1. **Generic statements** — "We offer the best tours"
2. **Tourism buzzwords** — "authentic", "unique", "amazing"
3. **Repetition** — Same content as other pages
4. **Thin content** — Less than 300 words of unique content
5. **Keyword stuffing** — Unnatural repetition of keywords
