## **AI Search Optimization: Keyword + Entity Strategy**

Per TJ Robertson's research, AI search cares about:

### **1. Entity Consistency (Use Same Names Everywhere)**

| Entity | Use This | Not This |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Founder | "Pauline and Maarten" | "the owners", "our team" |
| Vendor | "Aunty Lim", "Uncle Tan", "Pak Hassan" | "a local vendor", "an auntie" |
| Location | "Chinatown Petaling Street", "Chow Kit Market" | "the market", "this area" |
| Dish | "char kway teow", "nasi lemak", "laksa" | "fried noodles", "rice dish" |

**Action:** Create entity reference document for all content writers

---

### **2. Fact Density (AI Loves Specific Numbers)**

Add these statistics to pages:

| Page | Statistics to Include |
|------|----------------------|
| Homepage | "5,000+ guests since 2011", "4.9/4.9 rating", "520+ reviews", "max 8 guests" |
| About | "Founded 2011", "15 years", "career change from IT", "wedding gift story" |
| Tour Pages | "10-12 tastings", "6 vendors", "4-5 hours", "3-5km walking", "40-60 years vendor experience" |
| Vendor Stories | "Aunty Lim: 40 years", "Uncle Tan: 60 years, 3rd generation", "20-30% income from tours" |

---

### **3. Direct Answer Format (40-60 Words)**

AI pulls direct answers from first 2-3 sentences.

**Example FAQ Structure:**

```markdown
## Are Simply Enak tours halal?

Yes, Simply Enak tours are halal-friendly. We partner with JAKIM-certified 
establishments for Muslim guests, and clearly label all non-halal items 
(pork, alcohol) on mixed tours. Our halal tours feature authentic Malay, 
Indian Muslim, and Chinese Muslim vendors in Kuala Lumpur and Penang.
```

**Why This Works:**
- 47 words (in 40-60 word sweet spot)
- Direct answer in first sentence
- Specific details (JAKIM, locations)
- AI can cite this verbatim
