Key design decisions:
  • Info (white boxes): logistics + policies — Duration, Group, Schedule, Accessibility, Private/Group, Min Age, Cancellation
  • Good For (orange boxes): suitability tags — Walking, Language, Dietary, Family, Drinks, Local Impact
  • "No alcohol" shown as muted icon + strikethrough — communicates exclusion without implying it's missing
  • Family icon shows 3 sizes (adult + child + infant) — immediately recognizable
