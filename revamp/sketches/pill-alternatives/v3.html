Beyond Icons — 7 Different Approaches

What if we don't use icons at all? What if the metadata works differently?

1. Editorial subtitle — no icons, no pills text-first

Metadata sits directly under the H1 as a subtitle line. Orange for tonal items (time, dietary), white for structural. No icons, no boxes, no backgrounds — just typography. Reads like a book jacket or film poster credits.
2. Attribute bar — color-coded underlines editorial

Small uppercase label + value + colored underline bar. No icons, no background chips. The underline acts as the visual indicator — orange for "experience" attributes, white/grey for logistics. Like a specs table in an editorial layout.
