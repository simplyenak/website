Quick comparisons
Style A "Warung" — Most memorable and brand-distinct. Food references give it a story. Best if you want people to smile.
Style B "Batik" — Most culturally rich. Decorative dots feel Malaysian. Best if you lean into the heritage side.
Style C "Selamat" — Warmest, most human. Slightly imperfect feels genuine. Best for "Passionate Friend" voice.
Style D "Dua Warna" — Strongest brand identity. Uses red+orange as the only colors. Best for design system consistency.
Style E "Ringan" — Most practical. Smallest footprint, works at any size. Best if space is tight.
Style F "Rasa" — Bold and confident. Thick strokes feel solid. Best for a friendly, energetic brand.
My take

A "Warung" is the most Simply Enak — food metaphors for a food tour company make it uniquely yours. Nobody else would use a teh tarik cup for "time."

D "Dua Warna" pairs well with it if you want the brand colors to do more work. A's tea cup + D's red/orange coloring would be a hybrid.

C "Selamat" is the best match for "Passionate Friend" voice — imperfect, warm, human. But the food metaphors in A tell a richer story.
