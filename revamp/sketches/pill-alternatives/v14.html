Changes in v14:
  • Black background — no white anywhere. Boxes use subtle translucent backgrounds.
  • One option per box — English and Malay are separate, Halal and Vegetarian are separate.
  • Custom wheelchair icon — redesigned with seat frame connecting wheels, person seated in chair.
  • Walking bars: filled opacity 0.5, empty opacity bumped to 0.18 for better visibility on dark.
  • Label changed from "Moderate" to "Moderate Walking" — makes clear we're describing walking difficulty.
  • "No alcohol" uses Lucide CircleSlash (was Wine+strikethrough) — no food metaphor.
  • Gluten-free uses "GF" badge (was WheatOff) — no food metaphor.
  • Halal circle stroke opacity reduced to 0.35, removed transform:scale inconsistency.
  • Added "All Ages" badge variant for tours with no age restriction.
  • Info section title color bumped from #777 to #888 for better readability on dark.
  • Larger boxes on desktop (18px 28px padding, 80px min-width, 13px labels via @media).
  • All demo icons bumped from 20x20 to 22x22 for proportion with larger boxes.
  • Section title gap increased to 12px, margin-bottom to 28px for breathing room.
