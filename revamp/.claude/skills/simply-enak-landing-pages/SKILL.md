# Simply Enak Landing Page Mastery

**Expert system for creating and optimizing high-conversion landing pages for Simply Enak food tours.**

---

## 🎯 Core Purpose

Create landing pages that:
1. **Educate first** — Teach visitors about Malaysian food culture before asking for bookings
2. **Rank on Google** — Target geo-specific keywords with SEO-optimized long-form content
3. **Convert naturally** — Let visitors book because they understand the value, not because of pressure
4. **Sound like a friend** — Use the Simply Enak brand voice (The Passionate Friend archetype)

---

## 📋 Brand Voice Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### The Three-Feeling Test
Every page must make visitors feel:
- ✅ **WELCOMED** — "our Malaysia", "come as guest, leave as friend"
- ✅ **FASCINATED** — Specific stories, vendor names, generational recipes
- ✅ **CONNECTED** — Real relationships with vendors, not transactions

### Forbidden Words (Never Use)
| Don't Say | Say Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Adventure | Tour, walk, time with us |
| Journey | Tour, walk, visit |
| Discover | See, meet, taste, find |
| Explore | Walk through, visit, eat at |
| Experience (vague) | Tour, or be specific: eat, taste, meet |
| Immersive | (Just describe what happens) |
| Authentic | (Show through details: names, years) |
| Premium | Small groups, fair vendor pay, experienced |
| Luxury | (Just describe what's special) |
| Best | (Use proof: awards, ratings, reviews) |
| Amazing | (Sensory details instead) |
| Customer | Guest, friend, you |
| However | And, but, plus |
| Embark | Start, join, come |
| Unique | (Explain what makes it different) |

**Full list:** See `/var/home/maarten/website-optimization/revamp/WORDS_TO_AVOID.md`

### Writing Rules
- ❌ Never use em-dashes (—)
- ❌ Never use "it's not just X, it's Y" pattern
- ❌ Never use identity statements like "We're not tour operators"
- ✅ Use short, clear sentences
- ✅ Write like you text a friend
- ✅ Show, don't claim (specific names, ages, years > adjectives)

## 🏗️ Landing Page Structure (Educational-First Model)

### Standard Template (1,500-2,500 words)

```
1. HERO SECTION
   - Title: Educational hook (not sales)
   - Subtitle: Cultural context
   - Description: Story-driven intro
   - Primary CTA: "Discover [City]'s Food Heritage" (scroll, not book yet)
   - Secondary CTA: "See Our Tours" (for ready-to-book visitors)

2. HERITAGE INTRODUCTION
   - Title: "A City Built on [Industry], Shaped by Food"
   - 3-paragraph narrative: Immigration → cultural collision → fusion cuisine
   - Key facts: # of food stalls, markets, cuisines, hours of operation
   - Cultural influences: List all communities that shaped cuisine

3. FOOD CULTURES SHOWCASE (4 sections)
   - Malay Cuisine
   - Chinese Malaysian Cuisine
   - Indian Malaysian Cuisine
   - Peranakan (Nyonya) Cuisine
   Each includes: Description, key dishes, cultural context, image

4. SIGNATURE DISHES GRID (6-8 dishes)
   - Dish name
   - What it is + why special
   - Origin story (1-2 sentences)
   - Where to find it
   - Cultural significance
   - Image

5. NEIGHBORHOODS / FOOD DISTRICTS (4 areas)
   - Neighborhood name
   - Description
   - Known for
   - Vibe
   - Image

6. WHY FOOD TOURS MATTER
   - Tourist trap vs. local reality
   - What visitors miss without guides
   - Value of cultural context
   - Vendor relationships (14+ years)

7. OUR TOURS (Product Section)
   - Tour cards (2-3 max)
   - Each card: Name, location, price, duration, key highlights, CTA
   - Social proof near CTAs (TripAdvisor awards, ratings)

8. SOCIAL PROOF
   - TripAdvisor badges (Travellers' Choice 2023, etc.)
   - Specific testimonials (STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result)
   - Media features (NatGeo, Discovery, Lonely Planet logos)

9. FAQ (Objection Handling)
   - Vegetarian options?
   - Halal considerations?
   - Group size?
   - Weather policy?
   - Dietary restrictions?
   - Walking difficulty?

10. FINAL CTA
    - Headline: Warm invitation (not pressure)
    - Subhead: What happens next
    - Primary CTA: "Join Our Next Tour"
    - Secondary CTA: "Ask on WhatsApp"
    - Tertiary CTA: "View All Tours"

11. NEWSLETTER SIGNUP
    - Headline: "We write about Malaysian food..."
    - Email field
    - Subscribe button
```

## 🔍 SEO Requirements

### Keyword Strategy
- **Primary keyword:** `[city] food tours` (e.g., "Kuala Lumpur food tours")
- **Secondary keywords:** `[city] street food tour`, `food tour [city]`, `[city] culinary tour`
- **Long-tail:** `[neighborhood] food tour`, `[city] vegetarian food tour`, `private food tour [city]`

### On-Page SEO Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Primary keyword in 2-3 H2s
- [ ] Secondary keywords in H2/H3s
- [ ] 1,500-2,500 words total
- [ ] Internal links to `/tours/`, `/stories/`, `/about/`
- [ ] Images with alt text (include keywords naturally)
- [ ] Meta title: 50-60 chars, includes primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description: 150-160 chars, includes primary keyword + CTA
- [ ] URL slug: `/[city]-food-tours/` (not `/food-tours-[city]/`)
- [ ] LocalBusiness schema markup
- [ ] BreadcrumbList schema
- [ ] FAQPage schema (if FAQ section exists)

### Content Depth Requirements
Google needs 1,000+ words to understand the page topic. Target:
- **Minimum:** 1,500 words
- **Optimal:** 2,000-2,500 words
- **Maximum:** 3,000 words (diminishing returns after this)

## 📊 Conversion Benchmarks (Simply Enak Specific)

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Conversion Rate | 5-6% | vs. 2% homepage baseline |
| Time on Page | 3-5 min | Educational content keeps visitors |
| Bounce Rate | <35% | Strong hooks reduce bounce |
| Load Time | <2 seconds | Critical for mobile (60%+ traffic) |
| Mobile CTR | 3-4% | Sticky CTA required on mobile |

### CTA Best Practices
- **Above fold:** Primary CTA visible without scrolling (mobile + desktop)
- **Sticky mobile:** Fixed bottom bar with "Book Your Tour" (anti-pattern #10 if missing)
- **Trust indicators:** Near every CTA (TripAdvisor badge, "14+ years", etc.)
- **Objection handling:** Below buttons ("Small groups", "Free cancellation", etc.)
- **Form fields:** Maximum 4 fields initially (add more after interest expressed)

## 🎨 Design System Compliance

### Colors
```css
Primary: #b52d38 (Maroon) — Heritage, importance, primary CTAs
Secondary: #885e40 (Brown) — Tradition, authenticity, supporting content
Accent: #ffa333 (Orange) — Energy, highlights, trust indicators
```

**Usage Rules:**
- 60-30-10 ratio (60% white/neutral, 30% primary, 10% accent)
- No more than 3 brand colors per section
- Primary CTAs always use `#b52d38`
- Hover states: Darken by 10%

### Typography
```css
Headings: Merriweather (serif) — H1: 3xl-4xl, H2: 2xl-3xl, H3: xl-2xl
Body: PT Sans (sans-serif) — base-lg (18-20px for readability)
Line height: 1.85 for body text (optimal readability)
```

### Spacing
- Section padding: `py-16` desktop, `py-12` mobile
- Card spacing: `gap-6-8` desktop, `gap-4` mobile
- Max content width: `max-w-prose` (~65 characters per line)

### Components to Use
- `<GlobalHero>` — Hero sections
- `<Section>` — Page sections (variant: default/light/accent/dark)
- `<Badge>` — Category labels, trust indicators
- `<Button>` — CTAs (buttonType: primary-btn/secondary-btn)
- `<Prose>` — Long-form content (auto-formats headings, lists, blockquotes)
- Tour cards from `/tours/` page patterns

## ✍️ Copywriting Frameworks

### Hero Headline Formula
**Specific Result + Cultural Context**

❌ "Experience Authentic Malaysian Food"
✅ "The Malaysia Tourists Miss: Eat Where We Actually Eat"

### PAS Framework (Problem-Agitation-Solution)
**Use for:** Problem sections, objection handling

- **Problem:** "Most tourists eat at the same 5 restaurants on Jalan Alor"
- **Agitation:** "They never meet the auntie who's made laksa for 40 years, never learn why char kway teow became political, never taste the Malaysia we grew up with"
- **Solution:** "We walk you through neighborhoods where we actually eat, introduce you to vendors who've become family, and explain the stories behind every dish"

### Benefit > Feature Mapping
**Use for:** Tour descriptions, feature sections

❌ Feature: "We visit 8 food stalls"
✅ Benefit: "You'll taste 8 different dishes from vendors our families have known for decades"

❌ Feature: "Small groups of 8 max"
✅ Benefit: "You can actually ask questions and hear the stories, not just follow a flag"

### STAR Testimonials
**Format:** Situation, Task, Action, Result

❌ "Great tour! - John"
✅ "We were worried about finding vegetarian food in KL (Situation). Wanted something authentic but didn't know where to look (Task). Simply Enak took us to 8 vegetarian stalls we'd never find alone (Action). Best food trip of our lives — already booked our next tour (Result). — Sarah & Tom, UK"

## 📱 Mobile-First Rules

### Non-Negotiable Mobile Requirements
1. **Sticky CTA:** Fixed bottom bar with "Book Your Tour" (anti-pattern #10 if missing)
2. **Tap targets:** Minimum 44px for all interactive elements
3. **Form optimization:** Maximum 4 visible fields, proper input types
4. **Content density:** Less text per screen, more scrolling
5. **Image optimization:** WebP format, lazy loading, proper aspect ratios

### Mobile CTA Pattern
```html
<!-- Sticky mobile CTA (hidden on desktop) -->
<div class="sticky-mobile-cta">
  <a href="/tours/" class="cta-primary">
    Book Your Tour
  </a>
</div>
```

### Tier 5: Tour Detail Pages (Conversion-Focused)
- `/tours/kuala-lumpur-food-tour/`
- `/tours/vegetarian-food-tour/`
- `/tours/penang-food-tour/`

**Template:** Conversion-first structure (800-1,200 words)
**Goal:** Book this specific tour

**Structure:**
```
1. HERO SECTION
   - Tour name + location
   - Hero image (actual tour photo)
   - Key info: Duration, group size, price
   - Primary CTA: "Book Now"
   - Secondary CTA: "Ask on WhatsApp"

2. TOUR OVERVIEW (2-3 paragraphs)
   - What makes this tour unique
   - Vendor relationships (14+ years)
   - Specific dishes (6-8)
   - Cultural context

3. WHAT TO EXPECT
   - Duration, group size, walking distance
   - Dietary options, meeting point

4. ITINERARY / FOOD STOPS (6-8 stops)
   - Stop name, dish, story, context

5. INCLUSIONS / EXCLUSIONS
   - What's included, what's not

6. SOCIAL PROOF
   - TripAdvisor badge, testimonials, rating

7. PRACTICAL INFO (FAQ)
   - When, where, what to wear, weather, accessibility

8. PRICING
   - Clear pricing, what's included

9. AVAILABILITY CALENDAR
   - Embedded booking, next dates

10. FINAL CTA
    - Warm invitation, trust indicators

11. RELATED TOURS (Optional)
    - 2-3 alternatives
```

**Key Differences from Landing Pages:**
- Shorter (800-1,200 vs. 1,500-2,500 words)
- Single CTA (book this tour)
- Specific pricing (not "from RM X")
- Embedded calendar
- Urgency (next date, limited spots)
- More practical info

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns (Never Do)

1. ❌ Generic headlines ("Welcome to the Future of Food Tours")
2. ❌ Multiple navigation links (kills 15% of conversions)
3. ❌ Generic stock photos of people shaking hands
4. ❌ "Submit" as CTA text
5. ❌ Forms with >4 fields initially
6. ❌ No social proof above the fold
7. ❌ Vague value propositions ("We do food differently")
8. ❌ Anonymous testimonials ("Great! - J.")
9. ❌ Purple gradients on white (generic "AI-slop" style)
10. ❌ **Missing sticky CTA on mobile** (critical for food tour bookings)

## 🎯 Golden Rules

### Rule 1: Educate First, Sell Second
Teach visitors WHY KL food matters before asking them to book.

### Rule 2: Show, Don't Claim
❌ "Authentic Malaysian food"
✅ "Aunty Lim's laksa, same recipe since 1982"

### Rule 3: Specific Details > Generic Adjectives
❌ "Unique local flavors"
✅ "Tangy tamarind, fiery sambal, coconut milk so fresh it's still warm"

### Rule 4: Proof > Claims
❌ "Best food tour in Malaysia"
✅ "TripAdvisor Travellers' Choice 2023, 1,250+ guests, 4.9 rating"

### Rule 5: Friend Voice > Tourism Brochure
❌ "Embark on an immersive culinary journey"
✅ "Come eat where we actually eat"
