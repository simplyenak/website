# Linkable Stunt & Research Project Brief

Based on Caleb Ulku's insight (Jul 2026):
> "The future is viral linkable assets — stunts, research reports, newsworthy things that people write about. Not calculators (everyone does calculators). Something where people write about YOUR BRAND, not just the asset."

## Strategy for Simply Enak

### What makes a linkable stunt work
- Gets local/national media coverage
- Makes people write about YOUR BRINK (not just the data)
- Is newsworthy enough for editors to pick up
- Can be tied to Malaysian food/tourism culture

### Proposed Stunts

#### 1. "The 50 Hawker Stalls Project" (immediate, low cost)
- Visit 50 hawker stalls acrossMalaysiaKL/Penang/Melaka in 50 day
- Document each one: dish, price, story of the hawker
- Publish results as interactive map + ranking
- Pitch to: Malay Mail, The Star, Vulcan Post, food bloggers
- Why it gets covered: Original data, human stories, Malaysian pride

#### 2. "Malaysian Food Price Index" (quarterly)
- Track price changes of 20 iconic dishes across 5 cities
- Publish every quarter as data journalism piece
- Pitch to: Bloomberg, Reuters, local business media
- Why: No one else tracks food inflation at hawker level

#### 3. "The Dietary Directory" (evergreen + newsworthy)
- Comprehensive database: every halal-certified restaurant, every vegetarian-option hawker stall, every gluten-free-safe kopitiem
- Partner with tourism boards (MHTC, state tourism)
- Pitch to: Travel + Leisure, Lonely Planet, Time Out
- Why: First of its kind in Southeast Asia

#### 4. "Ghost Kitchen or Real?" (viral investigation)
- Investigate KL food delivery brands — which are real restaurants vs ghost kitchens
- Publish findings with names
- Pitch to: SAYS, World of Buzz, CILISOS
- Why: Consumer protection + controversy = links

### Execution Framework
1. Choose 1 project per quarter
2. Spend 2-3 weeks on research/data collection
3. Write the findings as a story (not just a list)
4. Create a dedicated landing page with interactive elements
5. Reach out to 10 journalists/bloggers with exclusive angle
6. Cross-post findings as infographic on social media

### Success Metrics
- Number of media outlets linking back
- Referral traffic from news sites
- Social shares of the stunt page
- GSC impressions for related keywords
