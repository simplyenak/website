# Monthly Press Release Strategy for Simply Enak

Based on Caleb Ulku's insight (Jul 2026):
> "If they're regularly mentioned on Google news sources, chat GPT is much more likely to pick it up. So we'll do that every month for every client now."

## Why Press Releases Matter for AI SEO

1. **Google News pickup = AI trust signal** — ChatGPT, Gemini, and other LLMs weight news mentions heavily when recommending businesses
2. **Entity establishment** — Regular news coverage builds your entity graph (Google's knowledge panel, AI understanding of who you are)
3. **Link building** — Journalists link back to your site when they cover your story
4. **Local SEO boost** — Local newspaper coverage is powerful for local ranking

## Recommended Services

| Service | Cost | Best For |
|---------|------|----------|
| **PR Underground** | ~$80/release | Google News pickup (budget option) |
| **PR Newswire** | ~$350/release | Wider distribution, journalist access |
| **EIN Presswire** | ~$50/release | Budget alternative |
| **Direct journalist outreach** | Free (time) | Best results, most effort |

## Press Release Template

```
HEADLINE: [Newsworthy angle] — [Simply Enak]

DATELINE: KUALA LUMPUR, [Date] —

[Lead paragraph: Who, what, when, where, why]

[Quote from founder/guide — human angle]

[Supporting data/facts]

[What this means for travelers/locals]

[Call to action]

About Simply Enak:
[2-sentence boilerplate]

Media Contact:
[Name, email, phone]
```

## Distribution Checklist

- [ ] Write release (500-800 words)
- [ ] Add 1-2 high-res images
- [ ] Include quote from real person
- [ ] Submit to PR Underground (or chosen service)
- [ ] Share on social media after publication
- [ ] Add "As featured in" section to website
- [ ] Track backlinks in GSC

## Measuring Success

- Number of news pickups per release
- Domain authority of linking sites
- Referral traffic from news sites
- Branded search volume increase
- AI recommendation mentions (manual check)
