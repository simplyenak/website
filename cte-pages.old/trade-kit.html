About Culinary Travel Experts

We have been running food experiences in Malaysia since 2010. Market tours, heritage walks, cooking classes. Over time, hotels and travel agents started asking us to run things for their guests — not because they wanted to outsource, but because their guests wanted things standard suppliers couldn't deliver.

A halal street food tour that also covered history. A gluten-free guest who wanted to cook Nyonya food. A corporate group of 40 with dietary notes covering four religions. We could say yes because we know the stalls, the kitchens, the guides.

Part of Simply Enak. Same team. Same 15 years of ground knowledge. Structured for trade partners.

Partners we work with: Four Seasons, Mandarin Oriental, Quintessentially, EXO Travel, and independent DMCs across Europe and Asia.

How We Work With Partners

1. Introduce Yourself

Tell us what you do, who your clients are, and what kind of food experiences you need in Malaysia. We'll send you this trade kit with sample itineraries, pricing structure, and terms.

2. First Proposal

When you have a client or group in mind, brief us. We come back with a detailed proposal within 48 hours. Specific restaurants, markets, guides. No commitment until you're ready.

3. We Run It

Dedicated guide, printed day-by-day, 24/7 ground support. Your client has a direct number for anything they need. Rain plan, dietary adjustments, last-minute changes — all handled.

4. Ongoing

After the first trip, you have a direct contact at CTE who knows your preferences. Future proposals get faster. You get first notice of new routes and seasonal availability.
