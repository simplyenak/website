Partner With Us

We only work with travel professionals. Hotels, DMCs, travel agents, food tour operators, corporate planners. If you send guests to Malaysia and they care about food, we are your local team.

How It Works

Four steps to working together.

Step 1

Introduce Yourself

Send us a note. Tell us what you do, who your clients are, and what kind of food experiences you need in Malaysia.

Step 2

Get the Trade Kit

We send you sample itineraries, pricing structure, destination overviews, and partnership terms. Review at your own pace.

Step 3

First Proposal

When you have a client or group in mind, brief us. We come back with a detailed proposal within 48 hours. No commitment until you are ready.

Step 4

Ongoing Relationship

After the first trip, you have a direct contact at CTE who knows your preferences. Future proposals get faster and more precise.
