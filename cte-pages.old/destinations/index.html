Where we work.

Every state in Malaysia has its own palate, its own hawkers, its own argument about whose version of a dish is correct. These are the places we know best and bring groups to most often.

Kuala Lumpur

Malay, Chinese, Indian, and Mamak food cultures on the same street, sometimes in the same meal. KL is where the whole country's eating converges.

Explore KL →

Penang

Hokkien mee, char kway teow, assam laksa. The same families at the same stalls for four generations. Hawker centres open before dawn, close when the food runs out.

Explore Penang →

Melaka

Chicken rice balls, devil curry, Nyonya laksa. A Portuguese-Dutch-Chinese-Malay kitchen collision. Compact enough to eat through in a weekend.

Explore Melaka →

Borneo

Sabah and Sarawak eat differently from the peninsula. Hinava with bambangan, wild fern stir-fries, bamboo chicken over open flame. Kadazandusun and Iban cooking that's hard to find without local knowledge.

Multi-city routes.

Most of our trips combine two or more places. Penang and Melaka share a coastline but eat nothing alike. KL connects to everything by road, rail, or short flight. Borneo is a separate chapter entirely.

We design routes around eating. Which markets hit on which days. Where to stop between cities. How to structure a trip so your group isn't eating the same style of food two meals in a row.
